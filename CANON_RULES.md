# CANON RULES

**Status: LOCKED**

## 1. Originality
This universe may evoke the pleasures of academy progression fiction—competition, measurable advancement, rivalries, friendships, elite institutions, hidden history—but it must not reuse protected characters, terminology, plot structures, factions, or power mechanics from another franchise.

## 2. Physics first
Resonance feels wondrous but remains rule-bound. Power cannot become arbitrary magic. Costs, vectors, system boundaries, storage limits, heat, injury, latency, and attention matter.

## 3. Character before spectacle
Battles reveal character. School life is not filler. Friendship, humiliation, study, romance, failure, class tension, family expectation and ordinary campus rituals make the extraordinary stakes matter.

## 4. Earn progression
Kade can possess a rare architecture without being the best fighter. Technical novelty is not automatic mastery. Every jump in capability must have an earned training, insight, sacrifice or relational cost.

## 5. No cheap resurrections
A death fake-out can occur only when seeded and mechanically plausible. Once a character is truly dead, reversal is forbidden unless the universe has explicitly established a cost-bearing mechanism beforehand.

## 6. Mystery ledger
Every major reveal must have at least two prior clues and must answer more questions than it creates.

## 7. Connected-universe discipline
Cross-series links should reward readers, not punish newcomers. Each series must stand alone emotionally and structurally.

## 8. Audio-first readability
Names must be aurally distinct. Action should remain intelligible when heard once. Dialogue voices should be distinguishable through diction and rhythm rather than typographic tricks.
