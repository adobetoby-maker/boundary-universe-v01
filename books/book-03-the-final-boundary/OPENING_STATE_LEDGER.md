# BOOK 3 — OPENING STATE LEDGER

**Status:** LOCKED FROM BOOK 2 ENDING
**Time:** immediately after Book 2 Chapter 34; thirty days before the provisional rendezvous/visibility window.

## World / contact state

- Humanity has undeniable first contact with a network humans describe as a survivor/continuation relation.
- The survivor's biological, machine, cultural, distributed, or mixed nature remains unknown.
- The countdown is provisionally understood as a geometry window that increases communication coherence and mutual detectability.
- The survivor has modeled rotating cells and sent the approximate concepts `TOGETHER / VISIBLE / CHOICE`.
- One pulse from the avoided-reference direction was independently verified by Lunar, Vahana, Helix, and the Refuge Node archive.
- The pulse was too short to decode and has not repeated.
- Quieters, Severance history, exact visibility mechanism, and the avoided source's identity remain unknown to the cast.

## Network capability

- Twelve-person interoperability has been demonstrated: Kade, Aya, Mara, Sera, Julian, Eli, Taren, Jonas, Arjun, Imani, Nessa, and Ren.
- Mature operation uses temporary scoped centers, task-specific Consensus cells, conventional handoff, and survivable withdrawal.
- No permanent mandatory center exists.
- Consensus still carries neurological and physical cost.
- Larger networks are not automatically safer, wiser, or stronger.

- Can hold task-specific shared models but accepts Aya and other operators can initiate without him.

- Relationship with Mara is real and capable of sharp disagreement.
- Relationship with Aaron is regular, difficult, and improving without resolution.
- Sera's physical absence is painful but not interpreted as abandonment.
