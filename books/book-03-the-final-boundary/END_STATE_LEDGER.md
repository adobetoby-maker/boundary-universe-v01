# BOOK 3 — PLANNED END-STATE LEDGER

**Status:** ARCHITECTURE TARGET. Update only when drafted canon earns a change.

## Civilization / contact

- Humanity survives the first Quieter suppression sequence without becoming a permanent single operator and without destroying all network Resonance.
- Quieters are understood as ancient, bounded safety systems created after the Severance, not a hateful conquering species.
- Humanity and the survivor establish a limited, revocable contact protocol.
- Earth is neither perfectly hidden nor continuously legible. It can create short, purposeful high-coherence events and return safely to plural operation.
- The revocable lattice becomes a public, multi-institution protocol with independent audit, local exit, and no permanent owner.
- Connected-universe futures remain possible but are not required to understand the trilogy's resolution.

## Kade Mercer

- Remains exceptional in architecture, not invincible in combat or entitled to command.
- Helps hold the minimum planet-scale layer and releases it before outcome certainty.
- Accepts a binding design in which Aya and regional operators can remove him.
- Final emotional state: can stay connected through fear, absence, and consequential disagreement without owning other people's choices.
- Does not become permanent center, planetary ruler, or sole public savior.
- Returns to Asterion life in a role that still leaves room for other students' beginnings.

## Mara Vey

- Co-authors and enforces the global layer's scope boundary.
- Makes a consequential choice Kade disagrees with; their relationship survives without either surrendering judgment.
- Remains Kade's partner and peer, not his conscience-as-service-role.

## Aya Mori

- Initiates the decisive separation proof and can sever the global layer.
- Receives equal public and technical authorship without hierarchy framing.
- Remains based in her own institutional/cultural context rather than being absorbed by Asterion.

## Sera Vale

- Leads civic continuity outside elite Consensus cells during the suppression sequence.
- Establishes an independent Kisiwa-linked response path.
- Returns by choice, not because Kade's story requires her physical presence.

## Eli Navarro

- Prototype Four's low-cost stabilization mesh preserves ordinary hospitals, transit, and local response during decoupling.
- His civilian engineering becomes a civilization-scale necessity without turning him into a magical universal technician.

## Julian Cross

- Uses conventional Resonance and scoped command to protect physical infrastructure while shared-state systems deliberately release.
- Remains excellent without treating excellence as ownership.

## Taren Holt

- Receives a bounded, witnessed operational key and uses it without exceeding scope.
- Survives. Trust repair ends the trilogy credible but unfinished; his future leadership potential remains optional sequel material.

## Aaron Mercer

- Survives. He contributes history and takes responsibility without dying as payment for forgiveness.
- Relationship with Kade remains imperfect but becomes chosen on both sides.
- Is no longer sole carrier of a secret or containment burden.

## Sen / Asterion

- Sen yields emergency authority into a public, challengeable structure and accepts consequences for prior concealment.
- Asterion remains a school, no longer a covert center disguised as one.
- The original last-place rank becomes a campus tradition about beginnings rather than a monument to power.

## Final image

- A new student with a poor record arrives at Asterion.
- The student sees `KADE MERCER — 10,482 / 10,482` preserved on the arrival board.
- The tradition communicates that rank records where someone began, not the boundary of what they may become.
