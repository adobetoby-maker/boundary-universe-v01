# BOOK 3 — SIX-PASS PRODUCTION PROTOCOL

**Status:** LOCKED FOR BOOK 3 DRAFTING

Every chapter must complete the passes in order. A pass may revise prose from an earlier pass; the final attestation records the evidence, not merely a `PASS` label.

## Pass 1 — Structure

Record:

- chapter promise;
- scene progression;
- payoff or irreversible end-state delta;
- relationship to act escalation;
- opening and closing state.

A chapter fails if it only moves characters/information into place for a later chapter.

## Pass 2 — Density

Test each scene for at least two active functions among plot, character, world, clue, relationship, consequence, school texture, and physics.

Required checks:

- no summary bridge where a consequential scene is owed;
- no repeated debate without changed evidence or cost;
- no technical explanation without human decision/consequence;
- no spectacle that leaves the state unchanged.

## Pass 3 — Character / Clue / Continuity

Verify voice, agency, inherited state, physics, names, institutional roles, family-clean language, and Rule 6.

Major reveals require two prior independent clues from different sources, scenes, institutions, or physical observations. A reveal must answer more than it creates.

## Pass 4 — Audio-first

Read for one-hearing comprehension:

- aurally distinct names and speakers;
- clear dialogue ownership;
- explicit spatial/action transitions;
- manageable sentence load in technical passages;
- acronyms and displayed text understandable in narration;
- no dependence on typography for meaning;
- no drafting or review language in prose.

## Pass 5 — Story Completeness

Confirm the chapter delivers the promised experience at sufficient scene depth. Word count cannot substitute for completion. Verify emotional processing, consequence, ensemble participation, and ordinary-life texture where required by the three-chapter school-life guardrail.

## Pass 6 — BSBC

Attest:

- **B — Book promise:** school relationships matter at civilization scale.
- **S — Specific progression:** the chapter makes a concrete, irreversible change.
- **B — Boundary rules:** physics, consent, agency, cost, and limits drive the outcome.
- **C — Character:** choices arise from distinct people, not plot convenience.

## Payoff-density gate

Before attestation, score the chapter on five axes from 0–2:

1. promise is dramatized rather than summarized;
2. progression contains at least two meaningful turns;
3. payoff receives consequence/processing before exit;
4. ensemble competence is distributed;
5. clue/reveal is fairly prepared and bounded.

Required score: **8/10**, with no zero. For a chapter containing a major reveal, axes 3 and 5 must both score 2.

## Attestation format

Each attestation records:

- manuscript word count;
- six pass findings and repairs;
- payoff-density score with evidence;
- Rule 6 clue IDs planted/advanced/paid;
- family-clean result;
- audio-first result;
- exact end-state delta;
- readiness for audio render.
