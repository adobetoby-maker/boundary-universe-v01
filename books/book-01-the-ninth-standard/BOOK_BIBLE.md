## Story promise
A reader should finish Book 1 feeling they lived at Asterion for a year.

## POV
Close third person, primarily Kade.
Limited alternate POV chapters/sections for Mara and Sen only when dramatically necessary.
No villain POV in Book 1.

## Kade Mercer
Age: 17
Opening status: Northline Alternative student.
Core competence: rapid situational modeling; unusual ability to redefine physical system boundaries.
Core wound: repeated institutional rejection has taught him to leave emotionally before he can be rejected.
False belief: needing people gives them leverage to hurt you.
Need: interdependence without surrendering agency.

## Mara Vey
Freshman rank at entry: #7
Drive: prove her success belongs to her rather than her family.
Voice: economical, precise, dry humor.
Relationship with Kade: contempt → curiosity → respect → trust → attraction.

## Elias “Eli” Navarro

# SCHOOL EXPERIENCE CHECKLIST

Each 3–4 chapter block should include at least two:
- class or academic pressure
- dorm/social life
- training culture
- rank movement
- friendship scene unrelated to immediate plot
- family contact
- campus ritual/tradition
- food/physical place memory
- romantic movement
- institutional politics

Never allow six consecutive chapters to be pure mission/mystery/combat.

---

# ABILITY LIMITS — KADE

At opening:
- can perceive transfers inconsistently
- cannot deliberately invoke boundary changes
- physical conditioning below academy median

Mid-book:
- can redirect through connected structures
- requires line-of-attention and intuitive system model
- overuse causes migraines, tremor, spatial nausea

Late-book:
- can temporarily define multi-person coupled systems
- requires trust/coordination
- cannot dominate another person's control without destabilizing the field
- Consensus is stronger when participants retain agency

**LOCKED:** Kade is not the strongest individual combatant at the end of Book 1.
