## D. Cross-Book Identity Finding — B2 "Calder" vs. B1 ch10 "Owen Park"

**Verdict: SAME CHARACTER. B2's "Owen Calder" is B1's "Owen Park," continued under B2's own rename sweep.**

Evidence FOR (same character):

- Same first name, same setting: B1 ch10 — *"Amadi assigned Kade to a boy named Owen Park, rank 2,804, who had the formal vocabulary Kade lacked and no patience for explanations that could not be expressed in symbols."* (chapter-10-vector-class.md:157) vs. B2 ch1 — *"Kade's group included Owen Calder again, a Vahana exchange applicant named Leena Das, and a quiet Perception student Kade knew only as Arun."* (chapter-01-the-countdown.md:1133) — same professor (Amadi), same class (Vector Analysis / logistics modeling), same partner relationship with Kade.
- The word **"again"** in "Owen Calder again" presumes an established prior pairing — consistent with the recurring B1 ch10/ch27/ch28 partnership, not a new introduction.
- Matching personality/dynamic: B1 ch10 — Owen has "no patience for explanations that could not be expressed in symbols," clashes with Kade's intuitive style. B2 ch2 — *"Owen Calder, two seats over, finished with twelve. Kade disliked him on principle."* (chapter-02-public-nine.md:181) — same adversarial-but-respected classroom dynamic.
- B2's rename sweep is independently confirmed to have touched exactly this kind of B1-inherited character: the ticket context states B2 already renamed "Owen Park→Calder, Jun Park→Seo, Dr. Venn→Kesler" as its own de-duplication pass — i.e., B2's authors already identified "Owen Park" as a name to fix and chose "Calder," which only makes sense if they were renaming the same continuing character rather than coincidentally reusing "Owen" for someone new.

Evidence AGAINST / caveats:

- No line in B2 explicitly states "the boy formerly known as Owen Park" — the identity is inferred from context (professor, class, dynamic, the word "again"), not stated outright. A grep for "Owen Park" inside the B2 tree returns zero hits — confirming B2 never uses the old name, consistent with a clean rename rather than leaving a bridging reference.
- B1's Owen Park is described as attending **House Sol** (chapter-10-vector-class.md:643: *"Owen headed toward House Sol"*); B2 does not restate his house affiliation in the passages found, so house continuity could not be independently confirmed from the text searched.

**Disposition:** B1 must adopt "Calder" for this character at the pre-print pass (see Collision Docket, item 7). Until then B1 correctly keeps "Owen Park" — audio for ch1–13 is published and ch10 falls inside that range, so this is NOT safe to rename now.
