# Rewrite Production Specification

## Intent

Re-author all thirty-three chapters of *The Ninth Standard* as a fresh edition
using the Fantasy Author A voice. Preserve the story, causality, reveal order,
character arcs, power accounting, family-clean policy, and downstream Book Two
compatibility. Do not perform a sentence-by-sentence paraphrase of the source.

The replacement must improve propulsion, emotional specificity, scene turns,
spoken readability, distinct dialogue, campus texture, and the visible human
cost of progression. A rare power is never a substitute for earned skill.

## Chapter acceptance

A chapter closes only when an independent editor and evidence verifier find no
remaining verified defect. Review must cover:

- canon, timeline, ranks, names, viewpoint knowledge, and reveal order;
- assigned chapter function, plants, payoffs, and ending pressure;
- causality, earned choices, progression receipts, and power costs;
- school-life density and relationship movement where applicable;
- action geography and intelligibility when action occurs;
- close-third viewpoint discipline and Fantasy Author A voice;
- family-clean compliance and mature-thirteen-year-old suitability;
- audio survival: a listener can follow speakers, action, terms, and transitions;
- repetition, stock phrasing, exposition load, and production hygiene.

The editor must preserve recorded strengths during repair. A preference without
a violated contract is a concern, not a repair ticket.

## Action-length policy

The ordinary chapter band is the architecture target plus or minus twelve
percent. A chapter designated for fight or embodied action may use an upper
tolerance of twenty-five percent when the additional space materially supports
action geography, escalation, tactical reversals, cost, choice, or aftermath.
This is permission, not a padding target: the editor must still file a length or
compression defect when the extra prose repeats beats, delays the turn, obscures
the action, or exists only to enlarge the chapter.

A scheduled major set piece must not be compressed merely to satisfy the
ordinary band. Its packet records the expanded ceiling and the owner's policy
exception so the author, editor, verifier, and archived run share the same
acceptance contract.

## Larger-scope gates

After Chapters 7, 14, 24, and 33, run an act audit covering thread debt,
plant/payoff inventory, relationship motion, progression distribution, repeated
scene scaffolds, and density. After Chapter 33, run a full-book audit covering
ending promises, canon and rank arithmetic, name collisions, family-clean text,
audio readability, metadata, and downstream continuity.

Only the locked rewritten manuscript may replace `../manuscript/`. The original
edition remains recoverable in Git history and on the branch base.
