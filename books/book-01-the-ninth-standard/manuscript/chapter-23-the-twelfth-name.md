# Chapter 23 — The Twelfth Name

Eli found the missing person by counting things nobody else had bothered to count.

“There are eleven,” he said.

Kade looked up from his food.

“Eleven what?”

“Public Standard Eights.”

“That is famously true.”

“No.” Eli turned his tablet around. “It is famously incomplete.”

Mara leaned closer.

The display showed old International Resonance Authority identifier blocks.

Eleven public names.

Twelve identifier numbers.

One missing record.

Kade stopped chewing.

Eli tapped the gap.

“Identifiers are assigned sequentially. They do not recycle them.”

Mara said, “So there was a twelfth.”

“Exactly.”

Kade already knew.

He did not want to know.

Eli cross-referenced the missing identifier with the precursor archive.

One partial match appeared.

**MERCER, AARON J.**

Classification history:

**STANDARD VIII — STATUS SEALED**

Kade stared.

His father had not merely worked at Asterion.

He had been one of the strongest known Resonants in human history.

And someone had erased him.

Mara said quietly, “Your mother may not have known.”

“I know.”

Eli said, “Kade, this means—”

“I know.”

He stood and walked to the window.

Asterion spread below them.

A school.

A research complex.

A monument built over thirty years of hidden decisions.

Kade pressed his palms against the glass.

“Sen knew.”

Mara did not argue.

“She had to.”

Eli said, “Probably.”

Kade turned.

“No more probably.”

His voice came out sharper than he intended.

He forced it down.

“I want the truth.”

Mara held his gaze.

“Then we need to decide which truth first.”

Kade frowned.

“About your father,” she said, “or about why the world thinks there were only eleven?”

The two questions should have been the same.

Kade was beginning to understand that they were not.
