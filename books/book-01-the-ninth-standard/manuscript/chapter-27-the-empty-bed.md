# Chapter 27 — The Empty Bed

Eli’s side of the room was too neat.

That was the first thing Kade noticed.

The second was the drone rotor on the desk.

Eli had promised to fix it three weeks earlier.

He never had.

Kade sat on his own bed and looked at the empty one.

People kept coming by.

Food appeared.

Messages appeared.

Nobody knew what to say.

That made two of them.

Mara came in once, saw him awake, and sat on the floor without speaking.

Sera brought Eli’s engineering notebook and placed it on the desk.

Julian handled the Meridian logistics Kade could not bring himself to think about.

Kade ignored almost everyone.

The old instinct had returned.

Close the doors.

Make the room smaller.

Need less.

It had worked before.

It did not work now.

Eli’s laugh seemed embedded in everything.

In the bent shelf.

The scorch mark on the workbench.

The dent in the ceiling from the drone incident nobody officially understood.

Kade stared at the empty bed and hated how much of himself had become attached to another person.

Then he hated himself for thinking that.

Sera found him there after midnight.

“You eating?”

“No.”

“That wasn’t a question.”

“Then why add the question mark?”

She put a bowl on his desk.

Kade did not touch it.

Sera sat beside him.

“He mattered,” she said.

Kade closed his eyes.

“I know.”

“Then let it hurt.”

He opened them.

“That’s your advice?”

“Yes.”

“Terrible.”

“Probably.”

He almost smiled.

Almost.

Sera stood.

“Memorial is tomorrow.”

Kade looked at the empty bed.

“I can’t.”

“Yes, you can.”

“I don’t want to.”

“That’s different.”

She left.

Kade stayed awake until sunrise.

For the first time in years, he did not tell himself none of it mattered.
