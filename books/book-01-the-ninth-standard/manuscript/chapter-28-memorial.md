# Chapter 28 — Memorial

Asterion held memorials in the Hall of Names.

Kade had walked past it dozens of times.

He had never gone inside.

Now Eli’s photograph stood beneath a wall of older names and older wars.

The room was full.

That surprised Kade.

Not because Eli had not been liked.

Because Kade had somehow imagined grief should be private.

A mistake, apparently.

Dr. Sen spoke first.

Then an engineering instructor.

Then Sera.

Then Mara.

Kade had not planned to speak.

He stood anyway.

For several seconds, nothing came.

Then he looked at the photograph.

“Eli broke things,” Kade said.

A small laugh moved through the room.

“He usually called it testing. That was generous.”

Another laugh.

Kade swallowed.

“He was the first person at Asterion who didn’t care why I was here.”

The room went quiet.

“He cared whether I ate. Whether I studied. Whether my footwork was terrible. It was.”

Mara’s mouth twitched.

Kade looked down.

“I spent a long time thinking people mattered less if you didn’t need them.”

He breathed.

“I was wrong.”

That was all.

It was enough.

Afterward, Kade returned to Meridian alone.

His tablet chimed.

A message had arrived on an old maintenance channel Eli once used for unauthorized drone tests.

Sender identification:

**NULL**

Text:

**DUDE.**

Kade stopped breathing.

A second message appeared.

**DO NOT FREAK OUT.**

Kade sat down very slowly.

Then a third.

**ALSO PLEASE DO NOT TELL SERA I SAID DUDE IN A FUNERAL WINDOW.**

Kade stared.

His hands started shaking.

Not grief this time.

Something dangerously close to hope.
