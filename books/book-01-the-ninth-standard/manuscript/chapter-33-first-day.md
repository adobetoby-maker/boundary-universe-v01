# Chapter 33 — First Day

Three months later, Asterion looked repaired from a distance.

Up close, Kade could still find the scars.

A replaced wall panel near Meridian.

A darker strip of flooring outside Lab Six.

A hairline repair across the arena once called the Mercer Fault.

He preferred that.

Perfect repairs would have felt dishonest.

The official story of the siege occupied four pages and said almost nothing.

The unofficial story occupied every conversation on campus.

Taren remained at Asterion under restricted status while the investigation continued.

Sera still had not forgiven him.

Neither had Kade.

But neither pretended the answer was simple.

Eli had become intolerable after surviving his own memorial.

“I am historically significant,” he reminded people.

“No,” Sera said each time.

Mara had reached rank four.

Julian remained number one.

Kade had not challenged either of them.

Not yet.

Aaron Mercer was alive, though almost nobody knew it.

His condition required medical care and continued containment work, but he no longer carried the entire system alone.

Kade had spoken to him eleven times.

Eight had ended badly.

That was, according to Elena, progress.

The new semester began with the rankings reset.

Thousands of students gathered in the central forum while the academy displays came online.

Kade stood with Meridian.

Eli bounced on his heels.

“You nervous?”

“No.”

Mara looked at him.

Kade sighed.

“Yes.”

“Growth,” she said.

“I regret learning things.”

The boards illuminated.

Names populated.

Kade found his.

**KADE MERCER**

**RANK: #37**

He stared.

Eli made a noise that should not have come from a human being.

Sera punched Kade lightly in the shoulder.

Julian, several steps away, called, “Still thirty-six places to go.”

Kade looked at him.

“You’ve been saving that?”

“For weeks.”

“Terrible.”

Mara was smiling.

Kade looked back at the board.

Rank thirty-seven.

He remembered ten thousand four hundred eighty-two.

He remembered pretending numbers did not matter.

The board flickered.

A second line appeared beneath his rank.

**STANDARD: IX**

The forum went silent.

Every display on campus changed at once.

Not hacked.

Not broken.

Overridden.

Asterion’s emblem vanished.

Coordinates appeared.

Then the countdown.

**00:364:17:42:08**

Eli whispered, “Is that days?”

Mara said, “I think so.”

Kade looked toward the Pacific.

Somewhere beyond the horizon, beyond Earth, beyond everything he had understood when he sat in Room Four complaining about a physics question, something had answered.

His tablet buzzed.

A message from Elena.

**Proud of you. Also eat something.**

Kade laughed.

Mara looked over.

“What?”

“My mother remains focused on the important threats.”

The countdown continued.

A year ago, Kade would have looked for an exit.

Now he looked around.

Mara.

Eli.

Sera.

Julian.

Even Taren, standing under supervision at the edge of the forum.

People who complicated everything.

People he would not trade for simplicity.

Mara stepped closer.

“So,” she said. “First day.”

Kade looked at the countdown.

Then at his friends.

“Could be worse.”

Eli stared at him.

“Something in space just sent us a countdown.”

Kade nodded.

“Fair point.”

The numbers kept falling.

And for once, Kade Mercer did not care where the exit was.
