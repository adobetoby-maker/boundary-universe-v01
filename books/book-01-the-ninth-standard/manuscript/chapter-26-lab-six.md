# Chapter 26 — Lab Six

The explosion happened six minutes after they left the archive.

It began as a flash under the eastern engineering wing.

Then the floor moved.

Kade hit the wall hard enough to lose his breath.

Alarms screamed.

Eli was gone.

One second he had been beside them.

The next, the corridor behind them had folded inward behind a pressure door.

Kade shouted his name.

No answer.

Sera grabbed Kade before he ran toward the smoke.

“Wait.”

“He’s in there.”

“I know.”

“He’s in there.”

“I know.”

Emergency teams arrived in under a minute.

That minute felt endless.

Mara stayed beside Kade while responders sealed the wing.

Taren vanished during the confusion.

Kade noticed.

He hated himself for noticing.

The casualty list posted two hours later.

Five injured.

Two critical.

One presumed dead.

**ELIAS NAVARRO**

Kade read the name until the letters lost meaning.

No body recovered.

The lab collapse had damaged the thermal chamber and flooded the lower level with field-stabilized coolant.

The official language was technical.

Mercifully technical.

Presumed dead.

Kade sat on the floor outside Meridian House because going inside meant seeing Eli’s empty room.

Mara sat beside him.

She did not speak.

Sera paced until her legs seemed unable to keep carrying her.

Julian arrived and stood across from them.

Kade looked up.

“If I hadn’t gone to the archive—”

Julian cut him off.

“No.”

Kade stared.

Julian’s voice was quiet.

“You do not get to make yourself the center of every bad thing that happens.”

Kade almost snapped at him.

Then he remembered Eli saying almost the same thing once as a joke.

That made it worse.

Kade looked away.

He had spent months learning that needing people made life larger.

Nobody had explained the other side.

Larger things left larger holes.
