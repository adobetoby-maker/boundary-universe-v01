# AUDIO NARRATION PROMPT — BOOK 1

## Default single-narrator direction

Single male narrator, natural audiobook performance. Contemporary American accent. Read with cinematic restraint rather than exaggerated character acting.

### Kade Mercer
Seventeen. Intelligent, dry, observant, defensive, with humor used to hide vulnerability. His dialogue should sound quick and effortless, while his internal narration is thoughtful and perceptive.

### Darius Bell
Slightly deeper, more physical and blunt, but warm underneath.

### Ms. Alvarez
Calm, competent, dry, and quietly invested in Kade; never maternal or sentimental.

### District proctor
Begins relaxed and mildly amused, then becomes increasingly controlled and unsettled.

## Performance rules

Keep character voices distinct through cadence and attitude rather than caricature. Narration should be immersive, confident, and moderately paced. Let jokes breathe briefly, but do not play them for sitcom laughs.

During examination sequences, gradually tighten the pacing and increase tension without becoming melodramatic. Slow slightly around important displayed terms such as **REDEFINE**, **000**, and major revelation paragraphs.

Read screen text distinctly but naturally, so the listener can recognize that it is displayed text without turning the performance robotic.

Chapter endings should land with restraint. The final line of Chapter 1 should be quiet, unsettled, and consequential—not triumphant.
