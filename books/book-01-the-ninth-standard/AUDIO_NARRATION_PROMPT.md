# AUDIO NARRATION PROMPT — BOOK 1

Single male narrator, natural audiobook performance. Contemporary American accent. Read with cinematic restraint rather than exaggerated character acting.

## Character direction

**Kade Mercer** — Seventeen. Intelligent, dry, observant, defensive, with humor used to hide vulnerability. Dialogue quick and effortless; internal narration thoughtful and perceptive.

**Darius Bell** — Slightly deeper, more physical and blunt, but warm underneath.

**Ms. Alvarez** — Calm, competent, dry, and quietly invested in Kade; never maternal or sentimental.

**District proctor** — Begins relaxed and mildly amused, then becomes increasingly controlled and unsettled.

## Performance rules

Keep character voices distinct through cadence and attitude rather than caricature. Narration should be immersive, confident, and moderately paced. Let jokes breathe briefly, but do not play them for sitcom laughs.

During examination sequences, gradually tighten the pacing and increase tension without becoming melodramatic. Slow slightly around **REDEFINE**, **000**, and major revelation paragraphs.

Read screen text distinctly but naturally, so the listener can recognize displayed text without a robotic delivery.

Chapter endings should land with restraint. The final line of Chapter 1 should be quiet, unsettled, and consequential—not triumphant.
