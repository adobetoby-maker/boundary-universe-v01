- Asterion was built around precursor/containment infrastructure; the school is real, but the site predates the academy identity.

## Consensus / Standard Nine
- Public display labels it `IX`, but **Consensus is not simply a ninth raw-power tier**.
- It allows multiple consenting operators to share one physical boundary model while retaining separate identity, intent, and specialized functions.
- Consent is structural, not ceremonial: passive presence or forced compliance does not create stable Consensus.
- Kade cannot command participants into it; he must create/hold the shared model and allow others agency.
- First stable modern network includes Kade, Mara, Sera, Julian, Taren, and Eli (via haptic/network bridge), with Aaron/Sen supporting containment context.
- Roles during climax: Mara field geometry; Sera safe load partition/route awareness; Julian force/prioritization balance; Eli machine/signal interpretation; Taren Directorate security lattice; Kade shared relationship/boundary model.
- A major temptation/failure mode is Kade trying to take centralized control; he deliberately loosens control and asks participants to choose again under stress.
