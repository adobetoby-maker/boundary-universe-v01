- Rank: **#37** at new-semester recalculation.
- Standard: publicly displayed as **IX** after the first stable modern Consensus event.
- Conventional measured field output remains atypical/near-zero compared with ordinary Resonants.

## Asterion / hidden history
- Contact Zero: damaged nonhuman artificial system found in outer solar system ~31 years before Book 1.
- Public Resonance history is incomplete; modern human Resonance science grew from study of Contact Zero emissions.
- Asterion was built around precursor/containment infrastructure; the school is real, but the site predates the academy identity.
- Refuge Node had been attempting to suppress/avoid transmission and is understood to have been hiding from something capable of hearing it.
- Continuity Directorate evolved from legitimate continuity planning into centralized-control/weaponization ideology.
- Competitive rankings also create continuous high-resolution capability measurement useful to hidden strategic selection/research.
- After the siege, international academy delegations from **Kiyomizu, Helix, Vahana, and Kisiwa** arrive early under an accelerated International Academy Exchange Season, seeding Book 2's school-world expansion.

## Consensus / Standard Nine
- Public display labels it `IX`, but **Consensus is not simply a ninth raw-power tier**.
- It allows multiple consenting operators to share one physical boundary model while retaining separate identity, intent, and specialized functions.
- Consent is structural, not ceremonial: passive presence or forced compliance does not create stable Consensus.
- Kade cannot command participants into it; he must create/hold the shared model and allow others agency.
- Expansion establishes explicit early failure modes: treating another participant as an object, flattening distinct people into one input, and allowing a high-confidence participant to become an accidental command center.
- First stable modern network includes Kade, Mara, Sera, Julian, Taren, and Eli (via haptic/network bridge), with Aaron/Sen supporting containment context.
- Roles during climax: Mara field geometry; Sera safe load partition/route awareness; Julian force/prioritization balance; Eli machine/signal interpretation; Taren Directorate security lattice; Kade shared relationship/boundary model.
- Each participant experiences and applies that competence as a distinct center inside the shared model; successful release is also voluntary and staged, proving separation is part of Consensus rather than a system failure.
- A major temptation/failure mode is Kade trying to take centralized control; he deliberately loosens control and asks participants to choose again under stress.
- All participants show neurologic/physical strain; post-event use is restricted pending study.

- New-semester Asterion displays are overridden by the same coordinates/countdown.
- Display shown: `00:364:17:42:08` (days:hours:minutes:seconds framing in-story).
- Aaron recognizes the countdown *format* as similar to one damaged precursor-memory sequence, but neither its source nor purpose is known.
- Exact meaning remains OPEN for Book 2.
