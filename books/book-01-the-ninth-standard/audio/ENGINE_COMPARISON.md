# AUDIO ENGINE A/B RUBRIC

Use the same manuscript revision and narrator concept for each engine. Do not compare different prose edits.

## Test set
- Chapter 1: humor → introspection → technical mystery → tension.
- Chapter 2: adult dialogue, mother/son emotion, institutional authority.
- Chapter 3: wonder, description, quieter emotional ending.

## Score 1–10

| Dimension | What to listen for |
|---|---|
| Narrator naturalness | Does synthesis disappear, or do you hear the engine? |
| Long-form fatigue | Comfortable after 30–60 continuous minutes? |
| Kade voice | Dry, quick, young without sounding childish? |
| Character separation | Distinct without caricature or obvious pitch tricks? |
| Emotional restraint | Serious moments land without melodrama? |
| Humor timing | Pauses support jokes without sitcom delivery? |
| Technical clarity | Rankings, numbers, Resonance terminology heard correctly on first pass? |
| Pronunciation consistency | Names/terms remain stable across chapters? |
| Editability | Easy to fix one sentence without changing surrounding performance? |
| Production reliability | Few artifacts, jumps, clipped words or random prosody changes? |

## Decision rule

Do not pick a winner from a two-minute demo. Listen to at least one full chapter from each engine and preferably 30+ continuous minutes.

If Google wins clarity/control but ElevenLabs wins performance, favor ElevenLabs for production narration and retain the Google SSML render as QA/reference audio.
