## Numerals and rankings

- `000` in dramatic score displays: read as **zero zero zero** the first time unless prose explicitly calls it “zero.”
- `#10,482`: read **rank ten thousand four hundred eighty-two** when clarity matters.
- Percentages should sound conversational rather than digit-by-digit unless displayed readout tension benefits from exactness.
