# ELEVENLABS AUTHORING RULES

ElevenLabs scripts are performance derivatives of the canonical manuscript, not canon.

## Principle

Use one primary narrator voice for the entire book. Let prose, punctuation and acting carry most character differentiation. Add performance cues only when the emotional intent is likely to be missed.

## Sparse cue examples

```text
[quietly]
For once, he had tried.

[short pause]
ZERO ZERO ZERO.

[controlled, unsettled]
"Don't touch anything."
```

Do not tag every paragraph or line of dialogue.

## Character direction

- Kade: seventeen, quick dry humor, emotionally defended; inner narration more vulnerable than speech.
- Darius: lower, blunt, physically confident, warmer than his surface suggests.
- Ms. Alvarez: calm, competent, dry, quietly invested; never sentimental.
- Eli: fast associative delivery; humor accelerates under stress.
- Mara: precise, economical, controlled; dry rather than icy.
- Sera: blunt, grounded, perceptive.
- Julian: easy confidence without aristocratic caricature.
- Taren: socially fluid and relaxed; later sincerity should contrast with his effortless charm.
- Sen: quiet authority; intensity comes from restraint.

## Dramatic text

For screen readouts, change delivery rather than shouting. `REDEFINE`, `000`, ranks and warnings should feel visually distinct in audio through a small pause and clean diction.

## A/B policy

For at least Chapters 1–3, keep Google and ElevenLabs renders from the same manuscript revision and compare them before choosing the production engine.
