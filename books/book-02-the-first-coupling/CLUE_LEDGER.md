## How to use this ledger

Each clue entry has:
- **ID** — unique reference for cross-checking (prefix FC = First Coupling)
- **Planted in** — chapter and approximate word position
- **Surface text** — what the reader sees; no hidden meaning signaled
- **Deep meaning** — what it will eventually confirm
- **Payoff** — where the reveal lands (chapter or book)
- **Status** — PLANTED / CONFIRMED (after chapter is written)
