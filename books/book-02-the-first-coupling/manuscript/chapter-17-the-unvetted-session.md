# Chapter Seventeen: THE UNVETTED SESSION

## Day 2,446

The authorization request entered Aaron's queue at 04:37 with three signatures and one empty field.

The empty field belonged to him.

He opened the packet in his office while rain moved against the courtyard windows in fine diagonal lines. The Geneva facility's original frames admitted weather according to standards established before field damping, pressure seals, or the expectation that a secure research building should not produce a draft across its senior specialist's left hand. A facilities request to replace them had spent eleven months circulating between historic-preservation review and counter-surveillance engineering. Neither office objected to the work. Each objected to the other's preferred glass.

The windows continued performing in their characterized state.

The packet authorized a practitioner-mediated readiness demonstration at Brno for 14:00 that afternoon. Dr. Celia Nwosu, Standard Five. Derived geometry only. Maximum drive energy 1.2 joules across two mechanically isolated reservoirs and one electrical return path. No source carrier. No non-Eight practitioner. No simulated Seven-shaped enclosure. Automatic closure at twelve milliseconds. Local medical and technical stops independent. All declared losses assigned to recovery, heat, acoustic transfer, controller work, or residual mechanical motion.

The operation would prove that Brno's Conduit lattice could support a human practitioner inside a bounded field relationship. It would not prove buffer transfer, source safety, secondary-practitioner capacity, cross-site aggregate control, or anything else the Continuity Council had placed under the phrase *active readiness*.

The request acknowledged those limits on page one.

Page two contained the reason it should not proceed.

Nwosu had completed passive fitting, two single-path Storage exercises, and one dual-path synthetic progression over nine days. Her declared release completed inside protocol limits every time. Neurological and field baselines remained clean. The automated summary marked all three operations green.

The raw release topology from the third operation carried an undeclared relation for thirteen milliseconds after the drive reservoirs closed.

No energy occupied it. No charge crossed it. No thermal debt accumulated. The relation joined the two permitted release paths after neither path retained anything left to release, a temporary piece of architecture without content. At millisecond fourteen it resolved without intervention.

The pattern fell beneath the reporting threshold because the progression protocol measured retained load, not relation persistence. Brno's independent reconstruction lead found it during manual review at 02:18. The lead entered a technical hold. At 03:06, the local board reclassified the finding as an in-tolerance adaptation response and released the hold by two votes to one. At 03:41, medical authority signed. At 04:12, the site director authenticated the packet.

At 04:37, it arrived for practitioner-interface classification.

The council reviewed active readiness in two days.

Aaron expanded the raw trace.

The Conduit interface did not add force to the mechanical release. Its drive mass supplied the impulse. The lattice defined which reservoir could exchange momentum with the receiving mass, when that exchange became permitted, and where the losses returned. Nwosu's Storage architecture held the permitted relation stable for eight milliseconds, then released it. The controller closed both reservoirs at twelve.

For thirteen milliseconds afterward, her field topology continued to represent the two paths as mutually relevant.

The relation did not match Amara's four-state sequence. It did not recruit a fifth state, reproduce a source transition, or imply the same mechanism. Calling it the same finding would violate the data.

Calling it nothing required less precision.

Aaron checked the authorization chain. The request named the operation *derived validation stage two, repeat with live routing*. The approved progression ended after stage two. Stage three required independent baseline review and seventy-two hours of observation before any additional practitioner-mediated exposure. Amara had written that dependency. Aaron had authenticated it. Kessler had accepted it.

The proposed demonstration acquired a new label and occupied the interval where the dependency should have gone.

His terminal chimed at 04:51.

**BRNO CONTROL REQUESTS CLASSIFICATION PRESENCE — PRE-ENABLE TEST 05:10**

Nine hours before the session.

Aaron opened a secure channel to the reconstruction lead.

Dr. Pavel Oravec answered from Brno's control room. Behind him, unfinished damping panels alternated with exposed concrete on the outer wall. The fourth site had reached that phase of commissioning when every visible surface either carried a certification mark or waited for one.

"Show me the thirteen milliseconds," Aaron said.

Oravec sent the native trace.

"I recommended seventy-two hours," he said. "The board's finding says the relation contains no load and resolves spontaneously. Both statements can be true."

"Did anyone alter your recommendation?"

"No."

"Did anyone ask you to withdraw it?"

"No. The site director asked whether the protocol prohibited proceeding. I said the signed progression does. Counsel said the demonstration falls under a separate validation authority."

"Who supplied that interpretation?"

"The under-secretary's readiness office."

Kessler had not removed a stop. She had found an adjacent route whose name did not include it.

Aaron requested the pre-enable diagnostic. Brno's empty lattice accepted a 0.2-joule mechanical impulse, permitted transfer across the first path, denied the second, and returned the unused fraction through the recovery assembly. Phase reconstruction reached Geneva after forty-six milliseconds. Absolute timing survived transit. Every declared system closed.

The hardware did exactly what it had been built to do.

The missing characterization belonged to the person they intended to put inside it.

"Keep the lattice empty," Aaron said.

Oravec's gaze shifted to a control outside the camera field. "Until when?"

"Until classification disposition."

"The current packet says pending, not held."

Aaron selected the empty field.

The system offered three dispositions: authorize, return for condition, deny.

He chose **DENY — PAIRING UNCHARACTERIZED**.

The control channel changed state. Brno's practitioner enable keys went dark.

"Now it says denied," Aaron said.

---

At 05:07, Kessler called.

She joined from the back seat of a Directorate car. Morning traffic moved in wet bands beyond the window, Brussels reduced to reflected signals and the occasional pale face of a building. She had already read his disposition. She carried the packet on a display beside him, open to the raw trace rather than the executive summary.

"You denied the session," she said.

"Yes."

"On the thirteen-millisecond relation."

"On the absence of a characterized pairing after it."

"The relation retained no load, produced no physiological effect, and resolved without intervention."

"Correct."

"Dr. Nwosu remains inside every published Standard Five tolerance."

"This is not a Standard classification review."

"It is a 1.2-joule derived operation below the maximum load she already handles in grid research."

"Grid certification does not characterize her interaction with Brno's Conduit geometry."

Kessler moved the packet to the progression authority. "The site counsel reads the demonstration as separate from candidate stage three. No simulated Seven architecture enters the boundary. No source-derived carrier enters the lattice. The seventy-two-hour dependency governs a different exposure."

"The dependency governs additional practitioner-mediated exposure after dual-path release."

"That wording does not appear in the stage-three section."

"It appears in the authorization graph."

"As a line between two boxes."

"Lines carry meaning. That is why we use graphs."

The car stopped. Red light moved across Kessler's face and held there.

"Could the session proceed safely?" she asked.

The question invited either false certainty or useful uncertainty. Aaron had spent enough time in classified rooms to recognize the available furniture.

"I have no evidence that it would injure her," he said. "I also lack the review required to classify the pairing."

"Those are not equivalent findings."

"No. The second controls authorization."

"The classification matrix gives you authority over practitioner-interface pairing to prevent an untested architecture from entering an active boundary. Nwosu has entered this interface three times."

"Under a progression that stopped when the new relation appeared. Repetition before the finding does not validate exposure after it."

Kessler let two seconds pass. "The council needs evidence that controlled programs can move faster than the external ones."

"Then it needs evidence we actually possess."

"A clean operation today would constitute evidence."

"A clean operation today would constitute an outcome from an unvetted pairing. It would not make the decision to run it vetted retroactively."

"And a failed operation?"

"Would strengthen the rule after spending the practitioner."

No heat entered his voice. Elevated threat-response indexing could produce heat later, when the call ended and his body received permission to register the exchange. During the decision it supplied clarity and a dry mouth.

Kessler read something from a second channel. "Nwosu has requested to proceed. She received the complete access-compatible finding at 03:22. She understands the Amara Sen persistence disclosure. Medical confirms capacity and informed consent."

"Consent permits a qualified risk. It does not qualify the instrument."

"You are using classification authority to prevent a competent practitioner from accepting an uncertain risk."

"I am using it to prevent the program from describing an uncharacterized pairing as readiness."

"The program can describe the uncertainty."

"After exposing her to answer it."

Kessler's car moved again. "Every validation exposure answers an uncertainty by exposing someone to it."

There lay the real argument, stated without disguise.

Conduits existed because no interface became safe by remaining empty. Soren's characterized protocol existed because he, Amara, and Aaron had entered bounded relationships whose outcomes the models could not guarantee. Aaron could not defend an absolute rule against uncertainty without invalidating his own work.

The distinction needed to carry weight.

"Yes," he said. "After the relevant questions are defined, the observation period closes, the reconstruction receives independent review, and the protocol says what result ends the operation. We do not know whether Nwosu's relation indicates adaptation, incomplete release, or an ordinary topology the reporting threshold concealed in prior work. Today’s packet has no prospective discriminator among them. It asks her to enter the boundary first and lets the result define what we meant to test."

"Oravec can write a discriminator by noon."

"Independent review cannot finish by fourteen hundred."

"Not before the council review either."

"Correct."

The word closed the schedule.

Kessler's attention returned fully to him. "Are you denying any practitioner-mediated Brno operation before Day 2,448?"

"Under the candidates currently screened, yes."

"Put that scope in writing."

"I will."

"And report in person this morning. Rhys's bridge room, 07:00. Dr. Nwosu attends."

"Agreed."

"Do not refer to her as unvetted in the meeting. She has nineteen years of Standard Five practice and two human-interface safety commendations."

"The pairing is unvetted."

"Then keep the grammar accurate."

Kessler ended the call.

Aaron sat with the dark channel for a moment.

She had defended the practitioner against the imprecision in his label while continuing to defend the session. The correction did not soften her position. It made the position harder to dismiss, which he suspected she understood.

He opened the denial and changed its scope line from **CANDIDATE UNVETTED** to **BRNO–NWOSU PRACTITIONER-INTERFACE PAIRING UNCHARACTERIZED FOLLOWING UNRESOLVED RELEASE TOPOLOGY**.

The longer statement occupied three lines and prevented nine convenient interpretations.

Institutional prose occasionally earned its mass.

---

By 06:42, the daytime security staff had opened the bridge room and activated its damping mesh. The former telephone exchange carried a colder temperature than the rest of the building. Secure systems rejected the wall's old heating ducts as uncontrolled penetrations, so climate control entered through two ceiling units that alternated between silence and personal criticism.

Aaron set the native trace on the main display.

Amara arrived first. She studied the thirteen-millisecond relation without greeting him, advanced backward through the release, then overlaid Nwosu's first two operations.

"It starts during dual-path closure," she said.

"At the second reservoir's final permitted transfer."

"Amplitude?"

"Below four ten-thousandths of baseline coupling gain."

"So the automatic system discards it."

"It preserves the raw trace and calls the session clean."

"A machine can be thorough and wrong in separate layers. Efficient."

She isolated the topology. Two completed release paths remained joined by a relation that carried no active transfer.

"It isn't mine," she said.

"No."

"Did anyone claim it is?"

"The consent packet cites your persistence finding as the unresolved comparable risk."

"Comparable in the sense that both continue after someone expected them to stop. So do meetings."

"The mitigation literature remains limited."

She saw the denied authorization. "You acted before calling me."

"Yes."

"Good."

The answer landed harder than reassurance because he had prepared to account for the choice.

"That should not become the default," he said.

"No. Today it was your authority and her pairing. My topology does not give me ownership of every unexplained relation a Five produces."

"Kessler believes consent and medical clearance support proceeding."

"Do you?"

"They support Nwosu's right to remain a candidate. They do not supply the missing classification."

Amara enlarged the release onset. "Then say that to Nwosu before you say it to the room."

"The meeting begins in eighteen minutes."

"You continue to discover time near deadlines."

She sent him Nwosu's direct channel.

Nwosu answered from a preparation room at Brno. She wore ordinary facility clothes rather than a practitioner garment; someone had canceled the medical preparation after Aaron's denial. A narrow locker stood open behind her. On its inner door, a child's drawing showed three people under a yellow sun whose rays reached the ground with admirable disregard for attenuation.

"Dr. Mercer," she said. Her voice carried fatigue without hesitation. "Am I receiving the technical version or the administrative one?"

"They should match."

"That has not reliably occurred this morning."

"The thirteen-millisecond relation leaves your pairing with Brno's interface uncharacterized. The session cannot proceed until we define prospective stop conditions, complete the observation interval, and obtain independent reconstruction review."

"I understand the relation. I signed the added consent."

"I know."

"Then you also know I requested authorization."

"Yes."

"Do you think I failed to understand the risk?"

"No."

"Do you think Kessler's office pressured me?"

"I don't have evidence of that."

"It did not. I asked to join this program eight months ago. I know what uncontrolled labs do with Storage practitioners because I have audited two of them. If Brno can demonstrate a safer path before those teams recruit someone, that matters to me independently of the council's calendar."

Her reasoning matched Kessler's premise because the premise corresponded to a real danger. Treating it as contamination would have made Aaron's answer simpler and less accurate.

"It matters to me too," he said.

"Not enough to let me proceed."

"Not enough to change what the data can support."

Nwosu considered him through the small delay between sites. "If the relation had not been found, you would have authorized."

"Subject to the complete review, yes."

"Then better instrumentation made me less eligible."

"For forty-eight hours, probably."

"And possibly longer."

"Yes."

"That creates an incentive not to inspect below threshold."

"It does."

"What are you doing about that?"

The question struck the part of the system his denial did not solve.

"Requiring native-trace review before every readiness disposition," he said. "Across all four sites, including previously cleared operations. No clean summary without topology review."

"That will delay more than me."

"Almost certainly."

For the first time, her mouth shifted near a smile. "Then at least I have produced scalable inconvenience."

"The Directorate tracks that as capacity."

The quiet weight at the end belonged partly to her. She accepted it with a brief movement of her head.

"I disagree with the denial," she said. "I understand it. I want the review started today."

"It has started. Oravec retains reconstruction lead. You select one of two independent reviewers. Kessler's office selects neither."

"Good. Do not use my name to imply I support the decision."

"I won't."

"And do not call me unvetted."

"The pairing is uncharacterized."

"Better."

The channel closed.

Amara stood beside the main display. "She likes you."

"No available measurement supports that."

"Some of us can work above the reporting threshold."

Aaron kept Nwosu's candidate record open.

The mask had lifted when she entered active progression. Nineteen years of practice occupied six hundred and twelve pages, most of it the ordinary evidence of competence: annual architecture maps, grid-buffer certifications, medical intervals, recovery curves, incident reports that ended with no incident. Her Standard Five topology favored relational retention over load retention. She held permissions cleanly and energy poorly, which made her unsuitable for resonant-material charge storage and exceptionally good at keeping complex systems from exchanging with the wrong destination.

Nine years earlier, a superconducting transfer station outside Malmö had lost controller agreement during a reservoir handoff. Three field paths opened against one another. Nwosu held the permission structure for twenty-one milliseconds while the physical breakers separated the reservoirs. She stored no current. The grid supplied and recovered its own energy. Her architecture kept the available charge from resolving across a path whose controller could no longer prove safe.

The commendation called the act intuitive.

Her technical statement used eleven pages to reject the term.

She described the indicators she had observed, the two invalid controller states, the remaining mechanical breaker latency, the thermal ceiling, and the exact point at which holding the relation would have become less safe than releasing it. She had understood the boundary and acted inside it. Nothing in the record suggested hunger, concealment, or a tendency to treat authorization as an obstacle designed for other people.

Aaron searched the file for a simpler reason to keep the lattice dark.

He checked her prior low-amplitude topology. Brno's instruments resolved four times below the threshold used in her grid certifications. A relation like the thirteen-millisecond trace could have occurred before without entering any report. He checked medication changes, sleep records, cognitive-load recovery, and the timing of her travel from Abuja to Brno. None supplied a causal candidate. He compared the trace against sixty-three public Five progressions and the restricted Geneva archive. Two public records contained unloaded relations after closure, but both followed active reservoir faults and both resolved with the fault. Nwosu's persisted after every declared system closed.

He found no disqualifying fact.

The absence made the denial harder and more necessary. A poor candidate could be stopped by pointing to the candidate. An excellent candidate required the institution to admit that competence did not repair a missing model.

Amara read over his shoulder. "You're trying to prove yourself wrong."

"I am checking whether the classification can change."

"With seven minutes left."

"Time remains a parameter."

"Occasionally even when inconvenient."

He opened the two comparable records. "If this is a common Five adaptation that older instruments discarded, the observation interval may establish it."

"And if it doesn't?"

"We write a protocol around an unresolved relation. Lower initial load, single-path closure before dual release, prospective topology threshold, immediate termination if the relation recruits an active reservoir or persists past its demonstrated range."

"So you expect to authorize her."

"I expect to review evidence."

"That is your optimistic phrasing."

Aaron closed the public comparison. "Would you have allowed the session?"

Amara gave the question the attention it required. "As scientific lead, no. As a Five reviewing another Five, I would want to. Her control is better than mine on derived systems. Her release discrimination is cleaner. If we need another buffer practitioner, she is the first candidate in this cohort I would keep."

"The thirteen milliseconds?"

"May be nothing. May be the instrument noticing how Storage actually learns a Conduit. May be an unfinished relation that becomes relevant only when a second practitioner enters it. We do not know, and today's operation would answer almost none of that."

"It would establish whether she can repeat a bounded dual-path release at Brno."

"Once. Under a schedule everyone in the room needs to call progress."

Aaron returned to the consent packet. Nwosu knew that Amara carried a persistent relation after a restricted, non-equivalent paired protocol. She did not know the relation reproduced the Node's final four-state sequence. She did not know it persisted after the paired signature helped translate a civilization-scale act of deliberate disconnection. Those facts remained inside the Mercer-Sen partition.

The restriction did not make Nwosu's consent false. It kept her from evaluating whether source correlation increased or decreased the relevance to ordinary derived work. Aaron had used the access-compatible uncertainty to support a stop while withholding the evidence that gave the uncertainty its weight for him.

Practice made the boundary easy to state.

It did not make the statement light.

"If she asks whether your relation matches hers, what do we say?" he asked.

"No. It doesn't."

"If she asks why we treat them as comparable?"

"Because both persisted beyond declared release and we cannot prove which restricted features caused mine."

"And if she asks what mine matches?"

Amara's attention stayed on the trace. "We tell her that answer remains source-restricted and let her decide whether the missing answer ends her candidacy. We do not imply mystery where classification will do."

Aaron added the question to the next consent review. The change could cost the program its strongest second-buffer candidate. It also gave Nwosu a way to refuse the unknown without requiring her to know what the unknown contained.

At 06:58, the ceiling unit started again.

"You used your authority to stop a person," Amara said. "Do not spend the next month converting her back into a protocol."

"That sounds operationally inefficient."

"You may note the exception."

At 06:59, the bridge room began authenticating participants.

---

Rhys entered in person. Kessler joined from her Brussels office. Oravec and the Brno site director occupied separate channels despite sitting fourteen meters apart, an independence measure that produced two encrypted transmissions and one shared wall clock. Nwosu joined from the preparation room. Varga represented medical authority. Directorate counsel completed the table.

Kessler began by asking Aaron to state his action.

He did.

"At 04:56 I denied authorization for the proposed Brno readiness demonstration. The denial applies to the Brno–Nwosu practitioner-interface pairing following an unresolved release topology. No practitioner-mediated operation at Brno may proceed before classification review closes. Current evidence cannot close it before the Day 2,448 council review."

The site director asked, "Are you classifying the thirteen-millisecond relation as an adverse event?"

"No."

"As harm?"

"No."

"As an incomplete release?"

"Unknown."

"Then what positive finding supports denial?"

"The positive finding is a persistent relation absent from the declared model. The denial follows from the uncharacterized pairing, not a claim of harm."

Counsel opened the concurrence matrix. "Under-Secretary Kessler's office argues the proposed operation occupies a validation route separate from the candidate progression. Does your authority reach that route?"

"My authority reaches the practitioner-interface pairing. Renaming the operation does not create a new pairing."

"Could another classification officer authorize it?"

"No officer with the required source-adjacent Conduit access currently holds final pairing authority."

The phrasing exposed the single point of failure again. Aaron did not use the vulnerability to dilute the answer.

Kessler addressed Rhys. "Does source-classification authority prohibit the session?"

"No," Rhys said. "The geometry is derived, no source carrier enters it, and the local aggregate monitor covers the declared operation. My office has no stop."

He did not lend Aaron an authority that did not apply.

Varga followed. "Medical authority finds no demonstrated harm and no contraindication. Dr. Nwosu has capacity, complete accessible disclosure, and an independent basis for consent. Medical has no stop."

One by one, the adjacent protections declined to make Aaron's choice for him.

Kessler brought up the Day 2,448 readiness definition. "Without a human operation, Brno cannot satisfy practitioner-mediated readiness. The council will therefore receive a negative finding."

"It should," Aaron said.

"External program one begins its first reported human-coupled calibration within an estimated six to twenty-eight days."

"That does not characterize this pairing."

"No. It characterizes the cost of delay."

"I accept that cost exists."

"Dr. Nwosu accepts it may exceed the risk of proceeding."

Nwosu spoke before he could. "I accept the risk for myself. I do not certify the pairing. Those are separate decisions, and I disagree with one of them."

Kessler inclined her head. "Accurate."

The correction preserved Nwosu's agency and Aaron's authority without reconciling them.

Kessler turned back to Aaron. "What would it take to reverse your denial today?"

"Evidence that cannot exist today. Seventy-two-hour observation. Independent reconstruction. A prospective model distinguishing adaptation from delayed closure. Stop conditions tied to both."

"If executive authority orders the demonstration?"

The room lost its minor sounds. Even the ceiling unit ended a cycle.

Aaron saw the structure clearly. An executive order could replace the concurrence matrix only by explicitly revoking it. Kessler could route the question upward, as she had routed the schedule around Rhys. The council could decide the competitive hazard justified changing governance. Aaron could then comply, resign, or force the institution to record which authority it had removed and why.

That remained different from authorization.

"Then the order must revoke my classification stop before the lattice enables," he said. "It must name Dr. Nwosu, identify the unresolved topology, state that the pairing remains uncharacterized, and assign responsibility for proceeding without prospective closure criteria. I will authenticate the technical facts. I will not authenticate readiness."

No threat occupied the statement. He had specified the interface through which the institution could override him. The interface required the decision to arrive without euphemism.

Kessler held his gaze from the wall display.

"I will not request that order," she said.

Rhys moved one hand from the closed folder before him. The motion offered no relief, only recognition.

Kessler continued. "The denial stands. Brno reports synthetic readiness and failed practitioner-pairing qualification on Day 2,448. The report will state that the failure results from an unresolved low-amplitude relation, not hardware deficiency or practitioner incapacity. Dr. Nwosu's review receives priority independent of the council milestone."

The site director started to speak. Kessler stopped him with the smallest lift of two fingers.

"The authority existed to be used," she said. "We do not call the system functional only when it agrees with the schedule."

Her premise had met the gate and stopped. Nothing about the stop converted her premise into error.

The meeting moved to consequences.

Native topology review became mandatory before clean disposition at all sites. Every candidate progression acquired a seventy-two-hour observation minimum after any undeclared relation, loaded or not. Executive dashboards would distinguish hardware readiness, synthetic readiness, practitioner qualification, and active authorization. The single green indicator Kessler's office had requested became four indicators, which reduced the probability that any meeting could describe the facility with one adjective.

At 08:11, the channels unlocked.

Nwosu remained long enough to select the Rotterdam reviewer. She did not thank Aaron. He did not require her gratitude to validate a stop imposed over her informed disagreement.

Rhys gathered his papers.

"You made them name the person," he said.

"The packet already named her."

"The schedule did not."

Aaron saw again the readiness column, the date, the empty authorization field. Institutions preferred pressure expressed as milestones because milestones had no nervous systems.

"Kessler could still ask the council," he said.

"Yes."

"She chose not to."

"Also yes."

"You sound disappointed by the absence of a simpler conflict."

Rhys considered this. "I have found simple conflicts expensive to maintain."

He left.

The main display returned to the raw trace. Thirteen milliseconds of unloaded relationship remained visible after every declared exchange had ended. The pattern might prove harmless. It might become a useful marker of adaptation. In three days, an independent review might support the exact operation Aaron had denied.

None of those future findings could qualify the decision before him that morning.

At 08:19, Aaron received the revised readiness dashboard. Brno showed green for hardware, green for synthetic control, amber for practitioner qualification, and red for active authorization.

The council would dislike the red.

It represented a person who had not entered the lattice.

Aaron authenticated it.
