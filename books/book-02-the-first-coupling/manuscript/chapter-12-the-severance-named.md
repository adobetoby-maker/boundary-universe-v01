# Chapter Twelve: THE SEVERANCE, NAMED

## Day 2,352

The sublevel corridor preserved the building's original water main behind glass.

Facilities had exposed the cast-iron pipe during the Conduit-room conversion, discovered that removing it would require opening two load-bearing walls, and classified it as architectural heritage. A small brass plate gave the installation date as 1911. Three meters away, a superconducting transfer bus passed through the same wall inside a ceramic sleeve due for recertification in nineteen months.

Geneva respected history most reliably when history proved expensive to move.

Aaron stood between the two systems at 06:47 and read Amara's twelve-day baseline summary for the third time.

The persistent four-state topology remained at 0.0063 peak source correlation, within two percent of its value before their third paired diagnostic. No fifth relation. No active Storage load. No identity correlation. No measurable change in energy accounting, task resolution, sleep architecture, memory, language, or executive function. The record supported stability across twelve days and three hundred fourteen ordinary Storage operations.

It did not support safety.

Amara had signed the new authorization at 05:58. Varga signed at 06:14. Rhys released the source aperture at 06:31. Aaron's field remained empty.

The proposed operation extended their prior diagnostic in two directions: depth and grammar. The Conduit would present the paired signature, replay the three transformations whose constraints Aaron could now predict, then hold the fourth state open long enough for the Node to continue. Amara would preserve every displaced relation across a staged Storage envelope. Aaron would couple the live source state, the translation lattice, and each envelope without allowing their incompatible definitions to collapse into one another.

Maximum source contact: four hundred milliseconds.

Their first paired session had lasted eighty.

The protocol called the increase **CONTROLLED EXTENDED SOURCE TRANSFORMATION 1**. It devoted nine pages to stop conditions and one sentence to purpose.

*Determine whether the source's paired-signature response contains a recursively testable transformation grammar beyond the currently characterized four-state relation.*

The sentence meant: find out whether the Node could make itself clearer.

It omitted the related question of whether clarity improved the situation.

Aaron authenticated the document. His signature occupied the last field again. Seniority had not made the field larger, only harder to leave empty.

Authorization existed because the third diagnostic had answered one narrow question without worsening the larger one. Renewed contact had not increased Amara's residue. It had also demonstrated that the acquired topology and the live source relation could occupy her architecture simultaneously without merging. That distinction made staged storage technically possible: one envelope for the known persistent pattern, three for incoming relations, each with independent return. The protocol did not treat absence of accumulation as evidence that accumulation could not occur. It treated one observed non-event as permission to test the next one under a lower ceiling than the equipment could sustain.

Rhys had accepted the logic after adding mandatory source closure at four hundred milliseconds. Varga had accepted it after adding language screening to the immediate baseline. Amara had accepted it after removing a sentence that described her participation as continuation of prior consent.

*New operation, new decision,* she had written in the margin.

Aaron had retained the sentence in the final protocol. Institutions preferred consent as a durable property because reacquiring it consumed time. Physics showed no corresponding preference.

The source sequence itself provided the final condition. Three prior paired operations had yielded a repeatable rule. The fourth state pointed toward material Aaron already carried at impression level and could therefore recognize without asking Amara to hold a wholly unbounded structure. Their proposed depth increased relational complexity, not source intensity. That distinction mattered to every stop model and offered no reliable comfort outside them.

Amara came down the corridor from Room Two, practitioner-gray sleeves pushed to her forearms. She stopped beside the glass panel and followed his attention to the old pipe.

"You're reviewing municipal infrastructure."

"It has a demonstrated containment record exceeding a century."

"And no recorded coupling events."

"An excellent safety profile."

She held out a paper cup. Aaron took it. The coffee met his established specification closely enough to imply either attentive friendship or a security failure. Current evidence supported both.

"Your authorization is still empty," she said.

"Not anymore."

Amara checked the status on her wrist display. "Then you have decided my twelve clean days outweigh your recommendation to stop."

"No. Twelve stable days satisfy the minimum evidentiary condition we wrote for reconsideration. Your decision, medical clearance, source authorization, and my technical certification remain separate inputs."

"You make agreement sound like a systems fault."

"Agreement can remove independent checks."

"So can being difficult on principle."

"That remains independently verified."

Her mouth shifted at one corner. Then she turned serious without a visible transition.

"What haven't you put in the purpose statement?"

Aaron read the brass date again. He could have given her the approved answer: the current transformation remained unclassified; additional structure might distinguish message from adaptive response. Both statements held. Neither contained the page he had written on Helix Perimeter and destroyed.

Amara knew the page existed. In Chapter Eight's preparation, he had referred to the session that produced it: civilization-scale coordination, deliberate cessation, an active orientation away from something that had not ceased. She did not know the page's language because no page retained it. She knew Aaron's formal account, which separated every impression from every supplied inference and stopped before the conclusion he had spent three years not entering anywhere searchable.

He could continue that arrangement. Practice presented the necessary sentences in correct order.

Practice also made the omission easier to identify while he constructed it.

"The fourth state approaches the topology from that session," he said. "Not the civilization-scale density. The transition from coordinated complexity to deliberate cessation."

Amara held his gaze. "Approaches how closely?"

"The first paired sequence preserved four of the original relations. The second established position and function as independent variables. Our last diagnostic confirmed the residue doesn't alter them. If the next transformation continues the same grammar, we may be able to distinguish what acted from what the civilization did in response."

"That distinction wasn't available before."

"No. I could tell that the cessation carried an internal signature. Chosen, not merely undergone. I couldn't tell what made choosing it rational."

The transfer bus behind the wall changed cooling mode. Its ceramic sleeve transmitted a faint contraction through the mounting frame. The pipe from 1911 contributed nothing and retained seniority.

"And you think the Node intends to show us," Amara said.

"I think it has constructed a sequence whose next valid state may answer the question. Intention remains outside the measurement."

"You think it trusts the paired signature."

Aaron had kept *recognition* out of the first report. He had kept *care* out of Soren's. The underlying observations remained complete; the human terms stayed with him, where they could not simplify anyone else's decision.

The terms had accumulated without becoming less human.

"I think it distinguishes what we do together from what I do alone," he said. "And it has offered the pair more structure than it offered me."

"That answer required fewer precautions than usual."

"The full set remains available on request."

"I have signed enough pages for one morning."

Amara opened the Room Two door. Before entering, she looked back at him.

"If the distinction resolves, don't protect me from the translation."

The request found a prepared location in him. Not because he expected it. Because he had expected to need a reason not to comply.

"I won't," Aaron said.

The answer committed more than the protocol recorded.

---

At 07:20, Conduit Rooms One and Two entered independent lockdown.

The old wall between them blocked air, sound, and every field interaction not explicitly admitted through the shared lattice. Nine authenticated contacts linked Aaron to Room One's Conduit. Eleven linked Amara to Room Two's Storage cradle. The interface verified their identities separately, then displayed the proposed joint boundary as two functions, five release paths, three local reservoirs, and one source aperture.

No diagram displayed trust.

Instrumentation had standards.

Varga occupied the medical station. Rhys observed from the secure suite upstairs, his channel limited to operation telemetry. Two technical leads held lattice and aperture stops. Kessler received delayed status only; no policy office needed live access to a question the equipment could not yet phrase.

Aaron stated his function.

"I will maintain compatibility among the source state, the local translation lattice, and four staged Storage envelopes. I will not supply missing relations, extend beyond the source-defined transformation, or substitute interpretive content into the outbound model. I close on grammar failure, identity correlation, undeclared exchange, unresolved return, or any independent stop."

Amara followed. "I will preserve displaced source relations in four sequential envelopes without translation, reordering, compression, or initiated transfer. I release on command, loss of boundary distinction, aggregate load above fifty percent, change in the persistent topology above twenty percent, fifth-relation recruitment, active retention, or any independent stop."

"Local accounting," the lattice lead said. "Bus reserve twelve point four kilojoules. Thermal ceiling three point one. Isolation stack charged from facility supply. No external energy path declared or permitted."

Every transformation would alter which systems could exchange state. Geneva would still pay for the alteration.

Aaron placed his hands against the dry contacts.

"Proceed."

The Conduit converted equipment into relationships.

Building ground became the permitted destination for charge equalization. The isolation stack became available work held behind a release condition. Coolant became a thermal return path. Amara's architecture entered as four nested capacities, each able to preserve a differential the next could accept without confusing origin or destination. The source aperture remained closed, indicated only by the precise absence where a relationship could exist.

Aaron joined the local systems.

Pressure gathered behind his eyes. The headache rose from two to four before source contact, a useful reminder that Standard Eight meant maintaining other systems' incompatible truths, not persuading them to share his.

"Joint boundary stable," he said.

"Storage load six," Amara reported. "All returns distinct. Persistent topology at baseline."

Varga confirmed both identity traces. The aperture lead gave source clearance.

Aaron opened contact.

The monitoring network resolved around him in ordered bearings: Geneva, lunar orbit, three remote facilities, the outer-system minimum. Beyond the instruments, suppression removed every ordinary signature a field structure of the Node's scale should have produced. The Conduit rendered that absence as a boundary condition rather than an object. Inside it, attention aligned to Aaron's carrier and then widened to include Amara's held function.

The Node answered with field-state change.

No sound entered the room. No image crossed the aperture. Eleven relations shifted at once, preserving the separation between Aaron's Coupling and Amara's Storage while placing both inside the larger paired term.

The opening completed in nineteen milliseconds.

Aaron submitted the first predicted transformation. Function remained invariant while position inverted. The Node returned the valid state.

The second prediction fixed position and exchanged which relation remained available to the pair. Again, the source followed the constraint.

The third prediction described the four-state sequence now present in Amara's baseline. Her acquired topology did not lead or amplify it. Live source state and persistent relation occupied different envelopes, distinguishable in origin and identical in order.

"Correlation point nine four," the translation lead said. "Three prospective matches."

"Storage twenty-one," Amara said. "Boundary clear."

Aaron opened the fourth envelope.

The Node continued.

The familiar sequence expanded from eleven relations to thirty-six without adding an energy path, a carrier identity, or anything the Conduit could render as human content. Amara caught the relations displaced by Aaron's attention and held them in their source order. For the first time, he could examine the deliberate cessation without losing the coordinated state that preceded it.

The difference sharpened.

What he had understood on Helix Perimeter as civilization-scale density now resolved into a repeated architecture: distinct self-modeling processes, each retaining its own boundary, coupled into shared physical decisions across separation. Not one mind. Not a network assembled around a central control. Many agents whose individual predictions participated in one boundary act without surrendering the distinction that made each prediction independent.

The Node represented the structure through recurrence at incompatible scales. Nine terms. Hundreds. More than the Conduit could enumerate before compression warnings entered the display. Each state preserved identity, relation, and shared consequence.

Humanity's public framework stopped at Eight because it had no operational category for what Aaron watched.

His private encoding contained one.

He did not touch it.

"Primary load thirty-eight," Varga said. "Headache?"

"Six."

The value carried no room for the part of his attention now maintaining compatibility between what the Node demonstrated and what human physiology could tolerate as an idea.

The transformation changed.

A new relation entered outside the coordinated many.

It carried no stable self-model Aaron could identify. No reflected address. No exchange offered to the paired signature. It existed as a recursive test applied across the larger topology: not asking what the coupled civilization meant, but measuring whether coupling could propagate beyond a local boundary.

The test found that it could.

The external relation acted.

Every permitted exchange among the coordinated terms narrowed at once.

Aaron watched the sequence remove options. Momentum could still resolve locally but no longer across the coupled boundary. Charge followed. Information-bearing state. Stored differential. Shared constraint. The process did not drain a reservoir or overwhelm the civilization with greater force. It redefined which systems could participate in one another until each mind, installation, vessel, and world remained physically present and relationally alone.

The first hundred terms went silent without disappearing.

Then the next hundred.

Then the scale exceeded enumeration.

Amara held every displaced relation the Conduit could not express. Her load climbed from twenty-eight to thirty-seven percent, not because energy entered her architecture, but because the number of intact return conditions multiplied faster than the interface could collapse them into categories.

"Aaron," she said.

"I have it."

He did not mean control. He meant the grammar.

The Node repeated the transformation under different initial conditions. A connected structure. A threshold test. A systematic reduction of permitted relation. Local systems persisted. The capacity to act together did not.

It repeated again.

Different scale. Same result.

Again.

The repeated initial states differed in more than count. One distributed coordinated action through fixed sites whose boundary conditions implied planetary mass and light-minute separation. Another linked mobile systems across intervals the Conduit represented through changing inertial reference. A third contained no stable material centers Aaron could identify at all—only self-modeling processes distributed through a field structure that treated location as a temporary parameter.

The Node offered no planets, vessels, bodies, or faces. Those images came from Aaron's mind and he discarded them as soon as they formed. What remained after imagery failed carried greater precision: in each case, separate agents maintained local agency; their shared architecture allowed a decision in one boundary to constrain physical resolution in another; the recursive test identified that reach; the acting process narrowed the permission set until coordination could no longer propagate.

At the smallest represented scale, some terms cut their own shared paths before external suppression reached them. Their individual processes continued. At the next scale, delayed severance trapped whole branches inside a boundary they could no longer alter together. At the largest, the number of lost relations exceeded the Conduit's compression range in six successive dimensions. The interface rendered the overflow as white discontinuities across the relational display.

Not deaths. The sequence contained no biological marker Aaron could translate as death.

Worse, in the category that mattered to the civilization. Each surviving term retained whatever existence locality permitted while losing access to the structure through which its civilization existed. A city could remain full of intact rooms after every door became a wall. Calling the building preserved would make a technically supportable statement and miss the event.

Aaron understood why the Node's record held absence as a positive condition. Nothing needed to vanish for everything connecting it to end.

The sequence carried no anger, appetite, punishment, or victory state. The acting relation measured a condition and suppressed it. An automated limiter could have produced the same emotional content, which meant none. The difference lay in scope. This limiter treated civilization as the unsafe process.

Aaron's earlier word—*cessation*—failed.

The civilization had not chosen to end. It had chosen to sever its own coupling before the external process completed the same operation without preserving a path back.

It broke the shared boundary deliberately. It partitioned what could still be partitioned, concealed what could still be concealed, and made its coordinated field state indistinguishable from absence. Some terms vanished from the active relation before the narrowing reached them. Most did not.

The absence that followed contained structure.

Amara preserved it.

Aaron could compare before, intervention, and after without any state dissolving into imagery. The transformation admitted a precise description: a civilization organized through coupled agency had cut its own defining relationships to evade a system built to eliminate unrestricted coupling wherever it emerged.

Not death as a single event.

Not surrender.

Severance.

The word came from Aaron. The topology supplied discontinuity, intent, and scale. His mind supplied a noun because human thought required somewhere to put the consequences.

The Node did not say it.

"Storage forty-four," Amara reported. Her voice tightened. "Returns distinct. No fifth relation in baseline."

The operation clock reached two hundred eighty-one milliseconds.

The sequence moved beyond the remembered event.

The external relation remained active after the coupled structure disappeared. It tested the quiet field again. Then again at a longer interval. Search without target. Suppression without anger. A process that treated silence not as innocence but as an unresolved measurement.

The Node placed its own reduced signature inside that tested quiet.

Aaron understood the orientation he had encountered three years earlier. The suppression did not merely conceal damage. It maintained an answer to a recurring question: no unrestricted coupling here. Nothing connected enough to require correction.

The paired signature occupied the same model.

For one interval, human Coupling and Storage stood inside the relation that distinguished silence from detection.

Aaron's threat-response indexing crossed from elevated to absolute.

"Close," he said.

The Node introduced one final transformation.

The acting relation multiplied across distance—not copies in a formation, not a single center extending control, but repeated processes sharing the same test. Aaron could not resolve origin, number, range, or whether the instances communicated. He could resolve function.

They made connected civilizations quiet.

His mind offered *suppressors*. Accurate and insufficient. *Hunters* supplied intention the sequence did not. *Executioners* supplied judgment. The nearest term named only the effect and left motive empty.

Quieters.

His word.

Not theirs.

"Release," Aaron said.

Amara opened the outer Storage envelope. Thirty-six relations returned into the local translation lattice in source order. The isolation stack spent 2.9 kilojoules preserving their distinctions through collapse. The second envelope released into the first, then the third into the second. Heat climbed through the coolant path: 0.42, 0.71, 1.03 kilojoules.

The fourth envelope resisted.

"Return four unresolved," the lattice lead said.

Aaron held the Node state, local lattice, and Amara's boundary compatible while the headache drove a clean line through his vision.

The unresolved relation contained no energy and no foreign identity. It contained the structural difference between a paired system and the quiet condition the external process tested for. Amara could not release it into a lattice whose model lacked a destination for the distinction.

"Destructive route," Aaron said.

"It will collapse the ordering," Amara answered.

"Authorized. Use it."

Her fourth envelope opened into the ceramic isolation stack without preserving source sequence. Geneva supplied 4.1 kilojoules to randomize the remaining relational order, paying in current and heat for the loss of information the operation required. The coolant return rose by two point three degrees. The distinction dissolved below instrument resolution.

"Storage clear," Amara said.

Aaron verified all five return paths, both identity records, the persistent four-state topology, and zero active load.

He uncoupled their paired boundary.

The Node held the final state without pursuit or resistance. The Quieters existed only in Aaron's translation now, a word attached to a function the field sequence had demonstrated and never named.

He closed the source aperture.

Remote bearings withdrew. Lunar orbit returned to passive reference. Geneva's lattice discharged into building ground. The Conduit removed the source from the room and restored the ordinary separations among chair, contacts, wall, and person.

The operation clock stopped at three hundred forty-six milliseconds.

"Primary identity confirmed," Varga said. "Storage?"

Room Two remained silent.

One second.

Two.

"Storage identity confirmed," the second medical lead said.

Then Amara spoke. "I am here."

Aaron kept his hands on the contacts until her voice and the identity trace occupied the same fact.

---

Medical separated them for fifty-three minutes.

Aaron's headache peaked at eight, accompanied by a twelve-minute language latency Varga described as mild because he could still name objects and severe because he could not reliably explain why anyone should care. Amara's Storage load reached baseline in nine minutes. Her persistent topology remained within three percent of its pre-session amplitude. No fifth relation appeared. No identity correlation crossed threshold. Memory, attention, and executive screens found no functional change.

The deepest contact they had attempted left every declared measure clean except Aaron.

This did not comfort him. It offended his expectation that consequential events should possess the discipline to mark the instruments.

At 08:31, Varga permitted a joint debrief in the secondary analysis room. The room still had no window. Its old brass floor rail ran beneath the secure display, a surviving provision for calibration weights nobody had stored there in decades.

Amara sat opposite Aaron with the raw topology between them. The display rendered thirty-six relational tracks, then reduced them to the eleven the current interface could retain without compression. The reduction removed scale first.

"Don't show me the summary," she said.

Aaron restored the full reconstruction.

Before translating, he checked whether the reconstruction deserved to be translated.

The lunar monitor and two remote apertures placed the thirty-six-relation expansion at the source sixteen milliseconds before Geneva's Conduit resolved it. Room One's outbound carrier contained the paired signature and three prospective models, but not the recursive test, the narrowing sequence, or any representation of deliberate severance. Amara's staged envelopes preserved source order while using fixed return geometry, excluding Storage organization as the origin of the changing pattern. Aaron's attention trace lagged each structural transition by between nine and twenty-three milliseconds. His mind could misname what arrived. It could not cause instruments separated by hundreds of thousands of kilometers to record the arrival first.

He removed the interpretive overlay and ran the transformation as pure topology.

Connection. Threshold. Narrowing. Isolation.

Four different starting structures entered the same functional sequence. The Node then placed its own suppressed state against the test and placed the paired human signature in the tested class. No word, image, or declarative symbol occupied any interval. The grammar nonetheless survived removal of every label Aaron had supplied.

The operation log carried three new limits. The trace did not establish when the Severance occurred. It did not establish whether the acting processes caused the conflict that preceded it or emerged from that conflict afterward. It did not establish whether the Node recorded direct experience, inherited memory, or a model assembled from other losses. Near-explicit communication did not mean complete history. It meant Aaron could now identify subject, condition, operation, and outcome without substituting an impression for any of them.

That exceeded every contact result before the paired signature.

It also produced a reportable fact large enough to damage any institution asked to hold it.

"I held the reduction," Amara said. "Not what it meant. I could distinguish repetition, and a change in what counted as connected. Then the return stopped fitting the lattice. Translate it."

Aaron had promised.

He marked the first structure. "Distinct minds acting through a shared physical boundary while preserving individual agency. Civilization-scale. The Node represented it at multiple orders of magnitude."

He marked the recursive test.

"This relation detects whether that form of coupling can propagate. When the test resolves positive, the acting process removes permitted exchange across the connected structure. It does not supply greater energy. It changes which systems can share momentum, charge, information-bearing state, stored differential, or coordinated boundary control."

Amara watched the tracks narrow to isolated terms. "Everything stays present."

"Locally. The civilization does not."

"And the deliberate cessation?"

"A countermeasure. They severed their own shared architecture before the process could identify and suppress every remaining path. The Node carries the record of that choice. The structure I translated as chosen cessation in the first deep session was the result, not the whole event."

Amara studied the long quiet after the last coordinated term disappeared.

"The Severance," she said.

Aaron considered the word in another person's voice. It retained the distinction he needed: an act, a wound, and the condition after both.

"Yes."

"What performed the test?"

"The sequence doesn't establish whether the processes are machines, minds, institutions expressed as machines, or something our categories divide incorrectly. It establishes repeated function. Detection. Classification. Suppression. Continued testing after the target goes quiet."

"Plural."

"Structurally. I can't give you a number."

"But you gave them a name."

Aaron had not spoken it. The term nonetheless occupied his translation with the settled quality of a conclusion whose alternatives had failed one by one.

"The Quieters," he said.

Amara's attention remained on the trace. "Did it call them that?"

"It called them nothing. There are no words in the sequence. Quieters is the least inaccurate term I have for a distributed function that makes connected civilizations indistinguishable from silence."

"Least inaccurate is doing unusual work."

"It has seniority."

The dry weight landed and remained between them. Neither moved it.

Amara advanced to the final transformation. "They are still operating."

"The Node's suppression responds to a repeated test represented after the Severance. That supports continued operation when this record formed. The live sequence presents the relation as current, but I can't translate the Node's temporal index into ours."

"You believe current means now."

"Yes."

"And a paired human signature appeared inside the detection relation."

Aaron enlarged the interval. The trace placed their combined function between the Node's suppressed state and the recursive test. It did not show detection. It showed comparability: the same class of relation at vastly lower scale.

"It showed that what we are doing belongs to the category the test measures," he said.

"Not that we crossed its threshold."

"No."

"Not that we are visible."

"No."

"Not that we remain invisible if the program scales."

Aaron did not answer immediately. Kessler's fourth site existed as conditional procurement. Three facilities already operated tier-certification pipelines. Rival groups held leaked revision 6.1 geometry and at least one forty-one-millisecond prototype trace. Each program pursued more stable interaction among more practitioners because progress in Resonance science pointed naturally toward connection.

No villainy entered the model. Only success.

"No," he said.

Amara leaned back. Twelve days earlier, she had chosen another exposure with the known risk placed in front of her. Now she knew the benefit's content. The persistent topology inside her remained stable, silent, and unexplained.

"Rhys will require a report," she said.

"Yes."

"Kessler will receive it."

"At her access tier."

"Which version?"

The secure display offered three report templates: operational, interpretive, and Contact Zero full-access. Aaron could enter the complete sequence, his translation chain, the named terms, and the projected scaling implication. The evidence supported every step if he preserved the confidence boundaries.

He could also report the source-confirmed existence of a recurring external suppression process, the historical loss of a coupled civilization, and a possible detection relationship requiring immediate containment review. True statements. Enough to change policy. Not enough to reconstruct what the Node had entrusted to the pair.

The distinction assembled itself with professional ease.

That ease had once protected him. Now it measured what three years of practice had made possible.

"The version required to stop further exposure while interpretation undergoes restricted review," he said.

Amara studied him rather than the display. "That is not the full version."

"No."

The answer cost him exactly what practice could not remove: the knowledge that she would understand the architecture because she had once built it around him.

"Will your report contain anything false?" she asked.

"No."

"Will it allow them to reach the same conclusion we reached?"

"Not all of it."

The room reauthenticated them. A green line traveled around the boundary, confirming two authorized occupants and no undeclared transmission.

Amara looked at the Severance trace again. "Then preserve what you remove."

Aaron opened a new restricted partition. He assigned two-person access: Mercer, Sen. He attached the raw reconstruction, translation chain, confidence map, and the words they had supplied.

**THE SEVERANCE**

**THE QUIETERS**

Below them he entered the first necessary qualification.

*These terms do not occur in the source communication. They are human translations of demonstrated field-state functions.*

The Node had communicated no language. It had altered relationships under controlled observation until Aaron could no longer pretend the result amounted only to an impression.

He saved the record.

For three years, the thing in the dark had maintained its silence because silence answered a test still running somewhere beyond human measurement.

Now two people knew what the test asked.

Aaron began writing the version for everyone else.
