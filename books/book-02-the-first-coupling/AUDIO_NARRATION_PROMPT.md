# AUDIO NARRATION PROMPT — THE FIRST COUPLING (Book 2)

**Production phase:** Post-Book-2-review (do not use until review complete)
**Format:** Guidance for audio narrator / TTS system

---

## Narrator character

Same register as Book 1: a precise, careful person describing precise, careful events. Three years on, Aaron carries more institutional weight — the narration should feel like it's coming from someone who has been doing this long enough that the extraordinary has become procedure, and who notices, privately, what that costs.

---

## Aaron Mercer's internal voice

Unchanged in mechanism from Book 1 — technical vocabulary standing in for emotion, delivered at the same pace regardless of what it's covering. New in Book 2: Aaron now withholds information from someone he respects (Amara) as a deliberate choice, not a reflex. Read those moments with the same flatness as everything else — the flatness is the tell, not an exception to it.

His mentorship of Soren introduces a new mode: patience with someone else's inexperience, delivered slightly warmer than his usual register but never sentimental.

---

## The Node's communications — escalating clarity

Book 1's field-state sequences were impressionistic, slower-paced, requiring the listener to build understanding alongside Aaron. In Book 2, the paired Aaron/Amara signature produces genuinely clearer contact — the sequences should still be read slightly slower than surrounding prose, but with more structural confidence, less groping-for-words quality, especially from Chapter 7 onward.

Chapter 12's Severance/Quieters disclosure is the clearest communication in the trilogy so far. Read it deliberately, without dramatic weight — the horror is in the content, not the delivery.

## Pacing rules

- Technical/institutional scenes (Kessler, Rhys, Directorate politics): measured, confident, no rush — this is people who are good at meetings.
- Coupling sessions: slower than Book 1's, but with more structural confidence in the delivery — clarity has a different texture than groping.
- Amara's residue moments (Ch9, Ch19): almost imperceptibly slower, a fraction of a beat longer between sentences. Do not editorialize.
- Chapter endings: same rule as Book 1 — 1–2 beats of silence after the final sentence.

---

## Vocabulary introduction

Carried over from Book 1 (assume listener fluency): Boundary Field, Standard Eight, coupling architecture, field-state sequence, Primary Coupling Interface.

New in Book 2 — first use slower, clear enunciation, subsequent uses normal:
- Conduit interface / Conduit Program
- Buffer / stabilizer architecture (Standard Five function)
- The Quieters (Aaron's own translated term — should sound like a term he coined, not an official designation)
- The Severance
- The First Coupling (title term — reserve its full weight for Ch24)

## Chapter-specific notes

**Chapter 6 (Past Authorization):**
The near-miss with Soren. This should NOT be read with the same devastating calm as Book 1's Yuki event — the outcome is different, and the delivery should carry real tension because this time Aaron is not too late. Let the relief register, quietly, at the chapter's end.

**Chapter 11 (Amara's Choice):**
Structural inversion of Book 1's "Amara's Version." Read Amara's questions to Aaron with genuine, unhurried curiosity — she is not afraid, she is precise. Her final "I'll continue" should land as flat and considered as anything Aaron says.

**Chapter 12 (The Severance, Named):**
The clearest Node communication in the trilogy so far. Read carefully, without dramatic emphasis. The content carries its own weight.

**Chapter 18 (Symposium):**
The Lena scene. Read with the specific ache of a conversation that could have gone somewhere and didn't. Not sad — restrained, the way Aaron restrains everything.

**Chapter 24 (The First Coupling):**
Title payoff chapter. When the term "The First Coupling" appears as the formalized doctrine name, give it the same unhurried weight as Book 1's "DISCOVERY" heading — this is a name that will outlive everyone who chose it.

## Production notes

- Background: none. Silence between scenes is silence.
- Music: none during chapters. Optional ambient tone between chapter breaks only.
- Maintain narrator continuity with Book 1 — same voice actor/TTS profile if possible, since Aaron's aging and institutional weight should be audible as continuity, not reintroduction.
