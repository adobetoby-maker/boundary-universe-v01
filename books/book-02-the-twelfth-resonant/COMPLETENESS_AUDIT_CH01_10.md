**Branch:** `draft/book-02-four-pass`
**Purpose:** verify that each attested chapter reads as a complete story unit, not merely a collection of plot beats.

## Completeness criteria
A chapter is considered complete when it contains enough of the following for its specific job:
- clear opening situation and immediate reader orientation;
- causal connection between scenes rather than unexplained jumps;
- lived context for new institutions/locations/technical ideas;
- protagonist and supporting-character motives legible in scene;
- failure/choice/consequence rather than summary-only progression;
- emotional or operational aftermath after the main turn;
- sufficient explanation of what changed and why it matters;
- clean handoff to the next chapter without requiring the next chapter to finish the current one;
- no lore claim presented as proven before the clue ledger supports it;
- no expansion merely to satisfy target word count.
