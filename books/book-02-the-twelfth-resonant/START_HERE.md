## Chapter drafting protocol — SIX PASSES
Every new chapter completes six passes before attestation and before the next chapter advances:

1. **Structure** — complete scene spine, chapter promise, causal chain, ending turn.
2. **Density / dramatization** — add lived scenes and world/relationship texture; no filler.
3. **Character / clue / continuity** — state-ledger consistency, supporting-cast agency, Rule 6 fairness, power accounting.
4. **Audio-first polish** — speaker clarity, audible scene geography, cadence, repetition, family-clean check.
5. **Story completeness** — read as a reader rather than as the drafter; verify setup/context, causality, motive clarity, lived proof, emotional processing, aftermath, standalone chapter shape, and next-chapter handoff.
6. **BSBC review** — transferable Brandon Sanderson + Bryce O'Connor / Warformed craft lens, without imitation. Verify Promise → Progress → Payoff, visible competence delta, limitation/cost-driven solutions, action geography before speed, distinct ensemble competence, no filler/repeated conflict, no artificial withholding, Rule 6 fairness, and a one-sentence end-state delta.

### Pass 5/6 repair rule
A short chapter is not automatically incomplete and a long chapter is not automatically complete. Identify a missing story function before adding prose. Repair with the smallest scene-level unit that solves the actual gap, then rerun affected continuity/clue/audio checks.

If no story function is missing, record `COMPLETE / NO REPAIR REQUIRED` and do not edit merely to produce a change.

### Payoff-density gate — LOCKED FOR BOOK 2
Before a major reveal, reunion, public demonstration, political reversal, battle, Consensus breakthrough, death/apparent death, or climax can advance:
- compare the scene-room given to the setup versus the payoff;
- verify the payoff is not merely summarized or interrupted at the moment readers have waited for;
- give major participants enough interior/operational experience to make the event feel lived;
- include consequence/aftermath before the next chapter replaces the event with a new escalation;
- use architecture word targets only as compression diagnostics, never quotas.

This gate exists because Book 1 external review found the dangerous pattern of fully dramatized setup and compressed payoff concentrated in later chapters. Book 2 must not repeat it.
