## Governing lesson from Book 1
Word-count variance is not itself failure. The dangerous pattern is **setup receiving room while payoff is compressed**.

For Book 2, ask of every major chapter:
1. Did the chapter's promise receive enough lived scene-time?
2. Did the competence/thematic turn happen on-page rather than in summary?
3. Did the consequence receive emotional/operational aftermath before the next chapter inherits it?
4. Is a large reveal being immediately replaced by the next reveal?
5. Is a target shortfall evidence of compression, or simply an efficiently complete chapter?

Architecture targets remain diagnostics, never padding quotas.
