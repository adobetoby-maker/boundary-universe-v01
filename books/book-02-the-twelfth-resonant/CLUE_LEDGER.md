# CLUE LEDGER — BOOK 2

**Status: FINAL through Chapter 34; see `FINAL_CLUE_SUPPLEMENT.md` and `FINAL_OUTSIDE_REVIEW_RECONCILIATION.md`.**

## Book 1 evidence inherited
- Consensus requires voluntary participation and preserves agency.
- Refuge Node was hiding and suppressing its signal.
- A 0.83-second signal escaped and received coordinates + countdown.
- Contact Zero history was fragmented/controlled by institutions.
- Aaron helped shape early Consensus research.
- Academy competition serves both legitimate education and hidden observation/selection functions.

## Book 2 promises to answer
1. Why multiple governments have incompatible versions of early Resonance history.
2. Why academy competition is unusually good at identifying Consensus-compatible people.
3. Whether Kade is necessary for Consensus.
4. What the returned coordinates/countdown represent at a practical level.
5. Whether the responder is a threat, ally, or something more complicated.

## Book 2 clues to plant before midpoint
- Different academies use suspiciously similar hidden cooperation metrics despite publicly different traditions.
- Kiyomizu archives contain a Zero-like diagnostic under another name.
- Aya can create synchronization effects without Kade.
- Helix historical models contain twelve-person topologies.
- Vahana / lunar records show unexplained pre-Contact Zero signal fragments.
- Aaron recognizes selection criteria that he should not know if Asterion acted alone.

## Book 2 midpoint payoffs
- Cross-academy Consensus works publicly.
- Governments realize consent-based network coordination can bypass existing command hierarchies.
- `Standard IX` is revealed as politically convenient terminology rather than settled science.

## Book 2 late payoffs
- Aya forms stable Consensus without Kade.
- Aaron admits helping design cooperation/personality selection embedded in academy systems.
- Multiple nations possessed separate indirect-contact fragments.
- Resonance's original function was primarily coordination/communication.

## Ending reveal boundary
Book 2 MAY reveal:
- responder is a survivor/descendant fleeing suppressive intelligence;
- countdown is a rendezvous/visibility window rather than a simple attack timer.

Book 2 MUST NOT fully reveal:
- complete Quieter origin;
- complete Severance chronology;
- final solution to civilization-scale Consensus;
- whether the responder can be fully trusted.

## Fairness rule
Every major reveal needs at least two independent clues from different institutions/characters. No single archive file may solve the cosmology.
