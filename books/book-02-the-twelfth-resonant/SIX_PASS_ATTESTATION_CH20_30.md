## Governing rule
A chapter advances only after:
1. Structure
2. Density / dramatization
3. Character / clue / continuity
4. Audio-first
5. Story completeness
6. BSBC review

Major reveal/climax chapters also receive a **payoff-density gate**: the promised experience must occur on-page with enough scene room and aftermath before the next chapter inherits the consequence.

A review pass that finds no manuscript defect is recorded as `PASS / NO REPAIR REQUIRED`; it does not create churn solely to produce another commit.
