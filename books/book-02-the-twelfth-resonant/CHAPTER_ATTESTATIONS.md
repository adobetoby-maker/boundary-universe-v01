# BOOK 2 — CHAPTER ATTESTATIONS

**Branch:** `draft/book-02-four-pass`

## Protocol
A chapter does not advance to the next chapter until it has completed four manuscript passes:
1. **Structure pass** — full scene spine, chapter promise, ending turn.
2. **Density pass** — dramatize missing scenes; school/world/relationship texture; no filler.
3. **Character + clue pass** — independent ensemble trajectories, Rule 6 clue fairness, continuity reconciliation.
4. **Audio-first polish pass** — speaker clarity, cadence, repetition, family-clean check, conservation/power-system check.

Each chapter is then attested against its architecture target. The target is an **expected production word count**, not permission to pad; a chapter may finish modestly above or below target if all scene functions are fully dramatized.
