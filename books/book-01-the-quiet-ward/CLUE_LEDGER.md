### Clues planned for Act I (Chapters 1–6)

**Clue: Standard instruments return null, not zero**
- Planned placement: Chapter 2 (Room Four)
- Distinction: zero would be a measurable minimum. Null is the absence of a reading. The instruments are not saying *nothing*. They are saying *I do not know what I am measuring*.
- Why it matters: instruments measure within known classification parameters. The null is diagnostic data about the limits of the classification system, not about Ruan.
- First payoff target: Chapter 11 (Field Mechanics) — Sable articulates this distinction explicitly in her private notes.
- Full payoff: Book 2, when a second practitioner encounters the same null and interprets it the same wrong way Sable initially did.

**Clue: Ruan stops before the useful information**
- Planned placement: Chapter 2 (Room Four)
- Presentation: he gives his history in careful sentences that stop at the moment where clinical data would begin. This is not evasion. He has told this story to multiple people who did not believe it, and he has learned to not give details that will be used to dismiss him.
- Why it matters: the pattern of what he stops before is itself a map of what was witnessed.
- First payoff target: Chapter 5 (History) — Sable notices the pattern and changes her questioning approach.
- Full payoff: Chapter 24 (Beginning) — he gives the full account.

**Clue: Field journal, page one**
- Planned placement: Chapter 6 (The Shape)
- Presentation: Ruan draws the same shape on the first page without being asked to draw anything specific.
- Why it matters: he has been trying to communicate this since the incident. He has not been given the right medium until now.
- First payoff target: Chapter 13 (The Shape, Continued) — Sable notices the shape is developing, not repeating.
- Full payoff: Chapter 24 — Ruan explains it is not a shape but a frequency.

### Clues planned for Act II-A (Chapters 7–14)

**Clue: Two prior null cases, closed under military authority**
- Planned placement: Chapter 7 (Standard Consult) — via Vey Orlan, oblique
- Chapter 15 (Military Authority) — via government health index, more specific
- Chapter 19 (The File) — direct confirmation
- Presentation: each layer reveals more, but each layer also stops short of full disclosure.
- Why it matters: Ruan is not unprecedented. Being not unprecedented is more frightening than being unique.
- Full payoff: Chapter 24 — Ruan confirms the other two were his rotation companions.

**Clue: Something large being careful with something small**
- Planned placement: Chapter 5 (History) — Ruan describes the incident sensation using this phrase
- Chapter 14 (Something Else) — Sable perceives the same quality of attention at the edge of her Standard Six range
- Why it matters: this is a description of the Node fragment's field behavior. It approaches carefully because it has learned from prior encounters that carelessness damages the entity it is approaching.
- Full payoff: Book 3 — the Node fragment's approach to Sable in Therapeutic Consensus space.

**Clue: You will know it when it happens**
- Planned placement: Chapter 8 (Ruan's Good Day) — Ruan asks if Sable has heard a signal she was not expecting. She says no. He says: *you will*.
- Chapter 14 (Something Else) — she perceives the attentive presence.
- Why it matters: Ruan is not predicting. He is reporting. He knows the field signature will eventually reach someone with Standard Six diagnostic range in a quiet facility.
- Full payoff: Chapter 14, and more fully in Book 2 when the signal becomes more legible.

### Clues planned for Act II-B (Chapters 15–19)

**Clue: BM-null designation**
- Planned placement: Chapter 15 (Military Authority)
- Presentation: Sable finds the designation in a public government health index. She cannot access the cases. She can read the suffix codes: BM-null-1, BM-null-2.
- Why it matters: the classification system has a category for this. The category is not public.
- Full payoff: Chapter 19 (The File) — she sees all three cases.

**Clue: Listening station**
- Planned placement: Chapter 16 (The Name) — Ruan says the contractor platform was a long-range passive listening station. He says that is what they told us.
- Why it matters: the qualifier *that's what they told us* does more work than Ruan may intend. A listening station is equipment. But what was it listening to, and why, and who commissioned it?
- Payoff target: Book 2 — the listening station's actual purpose and connection to Contact Zero history.

**Clue: The liaison's field frequency**
- Planned placement: Chapter 21 (After the Liaison) — Ruan says he could feel the field change in the hallway when the liaison was present. He describes it as *the same way you could feel me. But different frequency. Older. More practiced.*
- Why it matters: the liaison is a Standard practitioner of some classification. The fact that Ruan can perceive field states despite null instrument readings means his field sense is not absent — it is operating outside instrument range.
- Full payoff: Book 2 — the liaison is identified and his classification is established.

### Clues planned for Act III (Chapters 20–24)

**Clue: The chart disappears; the paper backup does not**
- Planned placement: Chapter 22 (Absence)
- Why it matters: the deletion was systemic, not thorough. The people who deleted the chart do not know about the paper backup, which means they do not know about Sable's clinical practice of maintaining physical records in ambiguous cases.
- Payoff: Chapter 23 (Still Here) — the paper record allows the patient to remain administratively invisible while still present.
- Full payoff: the paper record becomes the only legal documentation of the null field cases that remains outside government control.

**Clue: The rotation companions**
- Planned placement: Chapter 24 (Beginning) — Ruan confirms that the other two people in the null files are his rotation companions.
- Why it matters: they were all in proximity to the same event. Two were classified as non-restorable. Ruan survived with a different outcome. The difference is not established in Book 1.
- Payoff target: Book 2 — what made Ruan's encounter different from the others.
