### Clues planned for Act I (Chapters 1–6)

**Clue: Standard instruments return null, not zero** ✓ PLANTED (Ch 2)
- Planted in: Chapter 2 — Room Four
- Canonical text: BM diagnostic sequence; Sable's interior passage; chart note: "no reportable result. Null return on all four — no study generated. Distinct from zero-value finding; instruments returned no measurement rather than a measurement of absence."
- Fenn voiced the wrong frame ("burned flat, true zero") — the chapter declines to validate it
- Empty-room acquisition ran normal (confirming instruments work, deepening the null)
- Distinction: zero would be a measurable minimum. Null is the absence of a reading. The instruments are not saying *nothing*. They are saying *I do not know what I am measuring*.
- Why it matters: instruments measure within known classification parameters. The null is diagnostic data about the limits of the classification system, not about Ruan.
- First payoff target: Chapter 11 (Field Mechanics) ✓ DELIVERED — Sable articulated the distinction explicitly in private notes: "Not a measurement of absence. The absence of a measurement." Wrote "No instrument will resolve this case. I need to understand the field itself." Never entered in chart.
- Full payoff: Book 2, when a second practitioner encounters the same null and interprets it the same wrong way Sable initially did.

**Clue: Ruan stops before the useful information** ✓ PLANTED (Ch 2)
- Planted in: Chapter 2 — Room Four
- Canonical presentation: admissions interview; complete, well-made sentences that end at the line where clinical data would begin; "forty-one seconds" exists only as a number told to him; "I don't know what happened" delivered from outside the event as fact; pattern shown, not named by Sable
- Pattern: he gives what he has been able to give; he has learned where the line is by telling the story to too many people who used the details to dismiss him
- Why it matters: the pattern of what he stops before is itself a map of what was witnessed.
- First payoff target: Chapter 5 (History) — Sable notices the pattern and changes her questioning approach.
- Full payoff: Chapter 24 (Beginning) — he gives the full account.

**Clue: Field journal, page one** ✓ PLANTED (Ch 6)
- Planted in: Chapter 6 — The Shape
- Canonical presentation: Sable gave the journal Day 5 as a diagnostic tool ("write or draw anything that feels accurate"); Day 6 Ruan handed it back with page one drawn; Ruan's words: "It's what I was trying to say in the interview. I ran out of words, so I drew it instead."
- Shape description (canonical): "a pattern of lines, not closed, nothing joined to make a boundary or a figure... the lines had something that behaved like directionality, a way of moving that implied elsewhere, without any anchor she could fix it to... It looked like part of a larger pattern that was not on the page... a center of the pattern's gravity that was not the geometric center of anything, because there was no geometry to have a center."
- Sable photographed it on the intake scanner; charted with no-interpretation annotation
- Why it matters: he has been trying to communicate this since the incident. He has not been given the right medium until now.
- First payoff target: Chapter 13 (The Shape, Continued) ✓ DELIVERED — Sable noticed the shape is developing, not repeating; Ruan confirmed "It's not growing. I'm getting access to more of it. It was always this large." Private note: "The shape is larger than the page. Possibly larger than the medium." No edge visible. Shape predates incident.
- Full payoff: Chapter 24 — Ruan explains it is not a shape but a frequency. (DO NOT name it as a frequency in any chapter before 24.)

- Canonical private note 1 (verbatim): "Day 31. Clinical system shows bed as unoccupied. Ruan Sela physically present in Room Four. Care continuing via paper orders."
- Canonical private note 2 (verbatim): "The physical chart was not included in the administrative transfer. It is not in the system. It is not in the system because it has never been in the system — I have maintained it in parallel to the electronic record as a standard practice with ambiguous cases. - Transfer (NOT deletion) to restricted tier — she corrected "deletion" to "transfer" mid-thought in the prose
- "Held" — used as the GMA's own verb, callback to Day 12 letter

- Payoff: Chapter 23 (Still Here) — the paper record allows the patient to remain administratively invisible while still present.
