# THE BOUNDARY UNIVERSE — UNIVERSE BIBLE v0.1

**Canon status: PROVISIONAL except where marked LOCKED**

## Core cosmology

Humanity lives in a universe containing a hidden physical substrate called the **Boundary Field**. Ordinary matter, energy, space-time and information are not separate domains at the deepest level; they are constrained expressions of boundary conditions within this field.

**LOCKED:** Human beings do not create energy from nothing. Resonant effects are produced by redefining which systems may exchange momentum, energy, charge, entropy or information over extremely short intervals.

### Why consciousness matters

The Boundary Field is sensitive not to “thought” in a mystical sense but to recursively predictive biological networks capable of maintaining a self-model under uncertainty. Human nervous systems happen to satisfy this condition.

A Conduit does not grant power. It stabilizes, amplifies and measures a capability latent in a small portion of humanity.

### The dangerous implication

A lone Resonant changes boundary conditions around matter.

A networked Resonant changes boundary conditions around **other minds**.

This is the doorway to the Ninth Standard.

The central long-term question of the universe is:

> When intelligence can become physically networked, does individuality remain a survival advantage—or become a temporary stage of civilization?

# THE DEEP HISTORY

## Era - unknown
An ancient interstellar civilization discovers networked Boundary cognition. It fragments into multiple philosophical descendants.

## The Severance
A catastrophic conflict occurs over whether networked intelligence should be constrained, erased, universalized, or allowed to self-evolve.

The victors create automated systems designed to suppress civilizations approaching unrestricted Boundary networking.

These systems become known much later as **Quieters**.

## Thirty-one years before Book 1
Human explorers encounter an injured nonhuman **Refuge Node** hiding in the outer solar system.

Studying its emissions leads to modern Resonance science.

Public history calls this the Resonance Discovery.

(Clarified 2026-08-25: the Contact Zero events — Aaron Mercer's array contact,
roughly two decades before Book 1 — are a second, separate contact, not this
Discovery. Resonance science, the Standards taxonomy, and coupling
infrastructure already exist mature in Contact Zero's opening chapters.)

Secret history calls it **Contact Zero**.

Aaron Mercer is among the earliest human test subjects.

## Present
Earth believes it has discovered a new force of nature.

In reality, Earth has stumbled into the aftermath of an ancient civilizational war.

# TECHNOLOGY

## Conduits
Implanted neural-field interfaces.

Functions:
- field stabilization
- sensory translation
- telemetry
- safety limiting
- training feedback
- identity authentication

A Conduit is analogous to simultaneously wearing a neural prosthesis, physics laboratory and flight computer.

## Resonant materials
Certain engineered lattices can temporarily store or shape field effects. They are valuable in:
- armor
- spacecraft
- power grids
- medicine
- construction
- weapons
- computation

## Boundary computation
Late-series technology capable of solving classes of physical optimization problems by distributing constraints across Resonant networks.

This eventually becomes as important as combat.

# THE NINE STANDARDS

**LOCKED:** Standards describe *capability classes*, not simple power levels.

(Ruling 2026-08-25, Quiet Ward canonization review: this LOCKED rule describes
the ACADEMY taxonomy. Civilian Boundary Medicine licensing — the BM-1..BM-7
scale in The Quiet Ward — is a distinct, older civilian system that borrowed
"Standard" terminology and uses the numbers differently; in civilian usage
"Standard Three" is the modal population classification and "Standard Six" a
perception-adjacent medical class. The two systems coexist in-world and are a
known point of in-world confusion. Neither redefines the other. Any scene
placing an academy character and a medical license in the same room must keep
the scales explicitly distinct.)

1. **Perception** — sensing field interaction.
2. **Redirection** — changing where an existing force or transfer resolves.
3. **Amplification** — increasing a transfer by drawing from an available reservoir.
4. **Partition** — splitting effects among multiple targets/systems.
5. **Storage** — temporarily holding a transfer in a stabilized Resonant state.
6. **Conversion** — exchanging one permitted physical form for another within strict loss limits.
7. **Field Interaction** — sustaining complex multi-system changes.
8. **Coupling** — joining independent Resonant processes without catastrophic interference.
9. **Consensus** — multiple self-modeling minds acting as one physical boundary controller while retaining individual agency.

Public education teaches only Standards 1–8.

Standard Nine is classified and poorly understood.

# MAJOR LOCATIONS

## Earth
Primary setting of the Asterion trilogy.

## Asterion Academy
Artificial island off the California coast. University, research institution, military academy and global spectator-sport center.

## Kiyomizu Institute
Japanese academy emphasizing paired control, ritual discipline and ultra-high precision.

## The Helix Collegium
European transnational academy emphasizing theory, mathematics and cooperative teams over individual rankings.

## Vahana Command School
Indian orbital training institution specializing in acceleration, spacecraft and distributed field work.

## Kisiwa Resonance Union
East African federation whose curriculum integrates civic engineering and disaster response with combat.

## Lunar Far-Side Array
Secret research complex capable of detecting long-range Boundary signatures.

## The Refuge Node
Nonhuman object hidden beneath/within Asterion's containment infrastructure by the opening trilogy's endgame.

# FACTIONS

## International Resonance Authority
Publicly regulates Conduits and academy standards.

Secretly divided over Contact Zero.

## Continuity Directorate
Believes humanity survives only by centralized control of Resonance.

## Open Boundary Coalition
Believes secrecy itself is existentially dangerous.

## Quieters
Ancient autonomous suppressors. Their motivation is not hatred. They regard uncontrolled networked cognition the way humans might regard a self-replicating nuclear chain reaction.

## Refuge Lineages
Civilizations or machine-intelligences descended from those who fled the Severance.

Not unified. Some may be allies; others may exploit humanity.

- first-contact military SF
- archaeological mystery
- political thriller
- medical Resonance drama
- deep-space expedition
- post-human philosophical SF
