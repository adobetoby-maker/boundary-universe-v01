# READER CONTRACT AUDITS

Use these as release-architecture gates.

## R-001 — Three-Book Satisfaction Test
For each origin trilogy, assume no other Boundary Universe book exists.
- Does Book 3 resolve the lead's emotional arc?
- Does it resolve the trilogy-scale external conflict?
- Are the trilogy's promised mysteries paid?
- Does the larger-universe hook arrive after closure rather than replacing it?
- Would a reader recommending only these three books feel honest doing so?

Status: OPEN until each trilogy is architected.

## R-002 — Partial-Universe / ~13-Book Satisfaction Test
Test plausible non-completion reading paths, including approximately 13 origin-cycle books.
- Is every completed trilogy emotionally complete?
- Does an unfinished current trilogy behave like a normal unfinished trilogy rather than missing information from another series?
- Are cross-series references optional enrichment?
- Is there any point where a reader must leave one trilogy to understand another trilogy's ending?

Status: OPEN.

## R-003 — Fifteen-Book Origin-Cycle Satisfaction Test
Assume the ensemble saga is never published.
- Do all five origin trilogies form a satisfying macro-cycle?
- Does each lead remain necessary and distinct?
- Does each trilogy contribute one face of the larger problem without withholding local closure?
- Are remaining mysteries appropriately larger-universe questions rather than unpaid origin promises?

Status: OPEN.

## R-004 — Ensemble Book 1 Cold-Entry Test
Assume reader knows none of the origin trilogies.
- Can they distinguish all core leads/counterparts?
- Can they understand each ability at the level required for current action?
- Are present loyalties/conflicts legible without biography dumps?
- Can they follow and care about the central mission?
- Does the book make prior trilogies desirable rather than mandatory?

Status: BLOCKED until Ensemble Book 1 architecture exists.

## R-005 — Full-Universe Reread Test
Maintain a ledger of details whose meaning deepens later:
- symbols;
- historical claims;
- background news;
- artifacts;
- institutions;
- cameos;
- recurring measurements/frequencies/numbers;
- phrases that later acquire technical meaning.

Rule: reread rewards may reinterpret earlier material, but must never reveal that earlier books were incomprehensible without later ones.

Status: OPEN.
