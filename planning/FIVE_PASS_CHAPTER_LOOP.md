# FIVE-PASS CHAPTER LOOP — STORY COMPLETENESS GATE

**Status:** ACTIVE production governance for new chapter work
**Applies to:** all new chapters drafted after Book 2 Chapter 10, and any earlier chapter reopened for substantive revision

## Chapter state machine

`PLANNED → PASS 1 STRUCTURE → PASS 2 DENSITY → PASS 3 CHARACTER/CLUE → PASS 4 AUDIO → PASS 5 STORY COMPLETENESS → ATTESTED → AUDIO QC → LOCKED`

A chapter does **not** advance to the next chapter until all five passes are complete and attested.

---

## PASS 1 — STRUCTURE

Build the full scene spine.

Required checks:
- chapter promise is clear;
- all required scenes exist;
- causal chain works;
- chapter has a beginning condition, meaningful turn, and exit condition;
- progression is earned rather than announced;
- no treatment-density placeholders are being mistaken for scenes.

---

## PASS 2 — DENSITY / DRAMATIZATION

Make the planned events lived rather than summarized.

Required checks:
- missing scenes are dramatized;
- ordinary school/work/home/world texture exists where needed;
- supporting characters act for their own reasons;
- physical setting and action geography are legible;
- expected word count is treated as a diagnostic, never a padding quota;
- add scenes, not sentence-level filler.

---

## PASS 3 — CHARACTER / CLUE / CONTINUITY

Audit the chapter against the larger book and universe.

Required checks:
- character decisions match current emotional/state ledgers;
- supporting cast retains agency and independent trajectories;
- Rule 6 fairness is maintained for every planted clue/payoff;
- no reveal arrives before its earned clue threshold;
- power/cost/conservation rules hold;
- relationships do not reset for convenience;
- names, roles, titles, injuries, locations, and timeline are continuous;
- no accidental canon promotion from provisional theory.

---

## PASS 4 — AUDIO-FIRST POLISH

Make the chapter work when heard without visual support.

Required checks:
- dialogue speakers are clear;
- scene transitions are audible;
- action geography remains understandable aloud;
- repeated words/phrases are intentional;
- sentence rhythm and paragraph cadence work in narration;
- terminology/names are aurally distinct;
- family-clean standard holds.

---

## PASS 5 — STORY COMPLETENESS

Read the chapter as a reader would, **not** as its drafter or checklist owner.

This pass exists because a chapter can satisfy its architecture, continuity, clue, and audio requirements while still feel like an incomplete piece of story.

### The completeness questions

1. **Setup clarity** — Does the reader understand where we are, what is happening, why this scene/chapter matters now, and what the participants believe they are trying to do?
2. **Context sufficiency** — Is required prior information present or naturally recalled without recap dumping? Does a reader understand unfamiliar institutions, rules, stakes, or roles well enough to follow the chapter?
3. **Causal clarity** — Can the reader trace why each major event/decision causes the next one?
4. **Character motive clarity** — Do important choices make sense from the character's current knowledge and emotional state?
5. **Lived proof** — Does the chapter dramatize its important claim instead of merely stating the lesson/theme?
6. **Emotional processing** — After a meaningful success, failure, reveal, injury, conflict, or choice, is there enough human reaction for the event to land before the story moves on?
7. **Aftermath / consequence** — Does the chapter show what materially changed because of its central event?
8. **Standalone story shape** — Does the chapter possess a readable setup → complication → turn/payoff → exit, even while remaining part of a larger novel?
9. **Next-chapter handoff** — Is the exit strong enough that the next chapter feels caused or invited rather than simply adjacent?
10. **No false thinness rule** — A short chapter is not automatically incomplete. Expand only when a missing story function is identified.
11. **No false fullness rule** — A long chapter is not automatically complete. Length cannot substitute for missing context, causality, reaction, or consequence.

### Repair rule

When Pass 5 finds a gap:
- name the missing story function first;
- add or reshape the smallest **scene-level** unit that fixes it;
- do not pad surrounding prose;
- rerun affected continuity/clue/audio checks after repair;
- record the completeness-repair commit in the chapter attestation.

When Pass 5 finds no gap:
- record `PASS 5 — COMPLETE / NO REPAIR REQUIRED`;
- do not edit merely to make the pass produce changes.

---

## ATTESTATION GATE

A chapter may become ATTESTED only when:
- Passes 1–5 are complete;
- architecture promise is fulfilled;
- story-completeness questions pass;
- expected density is reasonable without padding;
- state/clue ledgers are current;
- any Pass-5 repair has been rechecked for continuity and audio clarity.

### Attestation checkpoint format

- Pass 1 — structure: `<commit>`
- Pass 2 — density: `<commit>`
- Pass 3 — character/clue/continuity: `<commit or review artifact>`
- Pass 4 — audio-first polish: `<commit>`
- Pass 5 — story completeness: `<commit or NO-CHANGE audit artifact>`

---

## NESTED PRODUCTION RULE

The fifth pass becomes part of every autonomous loop:

`chapter five-pass + attestation → next chapter`

`all chapters attested → book completeness/continuity audit → book attestation`

`all trilogy books attested → trilogy arc/reader-contract audit → trilogy attestation`

A later layer may reopen an earlier chapter if it exposes a genuine missing story function, but all repairs must be provenance-recorded and must not silently invalidate prior canon/clue state.
