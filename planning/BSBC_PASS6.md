# PASS 6 — BSBC REVIEW

**Status:** REQUIRED production gate after Pass 5 Story Completeness

BSBC = transferable craft review inspired by Brandon Sanderson and Bryce O'Connor / Warformed techniques, adapted to Boundary Universe canon and voice. It is a craft lens, not imitation.

## Position in chapter loop
`PLANNED → PASS 1 STRUCTURE → PASS 2 DENSITY → PASS 3 CHARACTER/CLUE → PASS 4 AUDIO → PASS 5 STORY COMPLETENESS → PASS 6 BSBC → ATTESTED → AUDIO QC → LOCKED`

## Checklist
- Promise → Progress → Payoff is legible at chapter scale.
- Training/action shows a visible before/after competence delta when applicable.
- Costs, limits, conservation, consent, information limits, latency, range and failure modes drive solutions.
- Action geography, ownership, knowledge and success conditions are clear before speed increases.
- Ensemble characters contribute through distinct competence; protagonist is not universal leader/interpreter.
- Earned competence surprises use familiar ingredients in a surprising arrangement.
- Every fight, drill, argument, hearing, investigation or secret changes story state.
- No conflict repeats without changing capability, knowledge, relationship, status, strategy or cost.
- No artificial withholding; withheld information requires an in-world reason that survives scrutiny.
- Rule 6 fairness remains intact; reveals answer more than they create and do not spend later-book reveals early.
- Story completeness remains intact: context, emotional processing, aftermath and handoff are sufficient for audio-first reading.
- End-state delta can be stated in one sentence.

## Repair loop
1. Freeze reviewed manuscript SHA.
2. Mark findings PASS / REPAIR / ESCALATE.
3. Repair scenes, not filler sentences, using the smallest sufficient edit.
4. Re-read changed chapter against the checklist.
5. Repeat until no material BSBC finding remains.
6. Record final manuscript SHA and one-sentence end-state delta.
7. Only then mark Pass 6 complete and allow attestation.

## Anti-imitation rule
Use transferable craft principles only. Do not imitate another living author's prose voice, character designs, terminology, protected settings, or signature expressions.
