# THE BOUNDARY UNIVERSE — MASTER KANBAN

**Status:** PROVISIONAL planning system
**Branch:** `planning/universe-kanban`
**Purpose:** provide one repeatable production board for completing the five origin trilogies, later discovery/prequel stories, and a ten-book ensemble saga without sacrificing per-book quality.

---

## 0. NORTH STAR

The Boundary Universe should eventually read as if five apparently separate worlds / genres / ability traditions were always parts of one deeper reality.

Readers should be able to enter through any origin trilogy and receive a complete emotional story. Cross-series connections should reward attention, never punish newcomers.

### Provisional macro-scope

| Cycle | Scope | Books | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Origin Cycle | 5 solo protagonist trilogies | 15 | Each hero earns a complete origin, worldview, cast, ability tradition, and trilogy-scale victory/cost. |
| Discovery Prequels | ~1 later-released prequel per origin line | ~5 | Reveal how each culture/institution first discovered, misread, named, or weaponized its special ability interface. |
| Ensemble Saga | Combined cast | 10 | Five leads converge, learn to combine incompatible-seeming abilities, and face civilization-scale Boundary problems together. |
| **Provisional total** |  | **~30 books** | Exact prequel count remains OPEN. |

**Important:** Do not force every book to the same word count. Architecture targets are expected production counts, not padding quotas.

# 1. KANBAN COLUMNS

Every ticket lives in exactly one state.

## BACKLOG
Interesting but not dependency-ready.

## DESIGN
Actively defining premise, canon, timeline, magic/ability mechanics, or trilogy architecture.

## READY
All upstream dependencies are satisfied. The next production action is unambiguous.

## DRAFTING
Book/chapter prose is being written through the four-pass loop.

## AUDIT
Chapter/book is undergoing density, clue, continuity, family-clean, power-accounting, and audio-readability review.

## AUDIO / PRODUCTION
Text is story-locked; only listening-driven fixes, copyedit, pronunciation, mastering, formatting, and release work remain.

## CANON LOCKED
Released-quality source of truth. Changes require explicit retcon/change-control ticket.

## BLOCKED
Cannot proceed until named dependency is resolved.

# 2. PORTFOLIO EPICS

These are the highest-level tickets. They should remain visible until the whole universe is complete.

## U-001 — Define the Five Origin Leads
**Status:** DESIGN

### Deliverable
Create five protagonist cards. One is Kade Mercer; four remain OPEN.

Each card must contain:
- protagonist name / age / home culture / profession or school-world;
- emotional wound;
- false belief;
- relational need;
- competence that is useful before powers matter;
- apparent local ability / “magic” model;
- actual Boundary-physics interpretation, initially hidden from characters/readers;
- hard limitation and cost;
- why this lead cannot solve another lead’s trilogy for them;
- what this lead contributes to the eventual five-person team that nobody else can replace;
- origin-trilogy genre promise;
- recurring supporting cast with at least three people who matter independently of the protagonist.

### Gate
Do not lock a new protagonist until the character works even if the connected-universe layer is removed.

---

## U-002 — Define Five Distinct Ability Interfaces
**Status:** DESIGN

### Goal
The abilities should initially feel like different magical/scientific systems, then later reconcile under the Boundary Field without collapsing into “everyone has the same power.”

### Locked requirements
- conservation / cost remains real;
- each interface privileges a different class of boundary relationship;
- vocabulary can differ radically by culture/era;
- local practitioners can hold partially wrong theories that remain useful;
- combined abilities should create emergent solutions, not additive superhero beams;
- no interface should make Consensus obsolete;
- each interface needs failure modes, training language, institutions, ordinary civilian uses, and moral temptations.

### Provisional design spaces — NOT CANON
Use only as prompts while designing Leads 2–5:
- path / probability / decision boundaries;
- identity / memory / information boundaries;
- biological / healing / self-model boundaries;
- spatial / threshold / topology boundaries.

Kade already occupies force/system-boundary and network-consensus territory. Avoid duplicates.

## U-003 — Five-Trilogy Standalone Test
**Status:** BACKLOG

1. It has a compelling Book 1 with no crossover knowledge required.
2. It has a full three-book emotional arc.

5. At least three crossover clues exist, but none are mandatory to understand the local plot.

This enforces Canon Rule 7: connected-universe links reward, not punish.

## U-006 — Release Architecture
**Status:** BACKLOG

Design the publication sequence separately from chronology.

Working principle:
- origin trilogies are the core books;
- discovery prequels release later, after readers care about the mystery they explain;
- ensemble series begins only after the necessary solo arcs are emotionally complete;
- prequels must deepen interpretation, not repair missing exposition.

Possible release pattern to test:
1. Origin Trilogy 1 complete.
2. Origin Trilogy 2 complete.
3. Origin Trilogy 3 complete.
4. Origin Trilogy 4 complete.
5. Origin Trilogy 5 complete.
6. Discovery-prequel cycle interleaved as strategic palate cleansers / revelation books.
7. Ensemble Saga 1–10.

Alternative interleaving may be tested commercially later, but chronology and canon must not depend on release tricks.

---

## U-007 — Discovery Prequel Cycle
**Status:** BACKLOG
**Depends on:** at least the relevant origin trilogy’s architecture and spoiler matrix

Working concept: later-released prequels about the first discovery, naming, institutionalization, or moral compromise around each ability tradition.

Each prequel must answer a question readers already care about.

Do NOT write a prequel merely because history exists.

For every prequel:
- identify the present-day mystery it pays off;
- identify the historical protagonist (not necessarily the origin hero’s parent/ancestor);
- establish what readers know that historical characters do not;
- establish what historical characters know that modern characters misunderstand;
- protect later ensemble reveals;
- end with a reinterpretation of something already seen in the origin trilogy.

Exact count remains OPEN; provisional portfolio assumes ~5.

---

## U-008 — Ten-Book Ensemble Macro-Arc
**Status:** BACKLOG
**Depends on:** U-001 through U-005 and all five origin-trilogy end states

### Ensemble promise
Five protagonists who each learned to master a different apparently separate reality-model must learn to operate as a band without one system or one hero becoming the universal answer.

### Provisional 10-book escalation spine — NOT CANON
1. **Assembly** — first forced cooperation; systems appear incompatible.
2. **Translation** — learn that each ability describes a different boundary domain.
3. **First Unified Mission** — partial combined success exposes a larger threat.
4. **Worlds in Collision** — home institutions/factions resist convergence.
5. **The Severance Echo** — history shows this kind of convergence has happened before.
6. **War of Methods** — allies fracture over centralization, secrecy, and risk.
7. **The Broken Band** — major loss/failure proves togetherness alone is not enough.
8. **Reassembly** — mature cooperation; role handoff and consent become operational at team scale.
9. **Boundary War** — multi-world/civilizational confrontation.
10. **The Common Boundary** — final synthesis preserves identity while allowing combined action; launches a new era rather than ending the universe.

### Locked ensemble requirements
- no permanent chosen leader simply because Kade appears first;
- leadership must move by problem/domain;
- every hero must solve at least one crisis the others fundamentally cannot;
- combined abilities must have interference costs;
- personal relationships must survive disagreement;
- the group should eventually feel like a band: shared rituals, arguments, private jokes, grief history, operational trust.

# 5. REUSABLE BOOK PRODUCTION LOOP

Every book, including ensemble books and prequels, runs this exact loop.

## B-001 — Book Promise
One-sentence reader promise; one-sentence emotional question; one-sentence escalation.

## B-002 — Opening State Ledger
Every major character’s starting emotional, relational, physical, political, ability, and knowledge state.

## B-003 — End-State Ledger
Define what must be true by the final chapter.

## B-004 — Mystery / Clue Ledger
Every major reveal gets 2+ prior clues from independent sources where possible.

## B-005 — Chapter Architecture
Usually 30–40 chapters unless the story wants otherwise.
For each chapter:
- title;
- expected word count;
- scene promise;
- character turn;
- progression turn;
- clue/payoff job;
- school/ordinary-life job where relevant;
- chapter exit.

## B-006 — Word-Budget Sanity Test
Compare architecture target to actual intended scene count.
Reject 1,500-word “chapters” that contain three scenes plus a relationship turn unless brevity is intentionally the form.

## B-007 — Draft Chapter 4 Times
Run four full revisions of the same chapter:
1. structural scene spine;
2. density / dramatization;
3. character + clue + continuity;
4. audio-first line polish.

## B-008 — Chapter Attestation
Before advancing:
- expected word count achieved closely enough without padding;
- all planned scenes dramatized;
- progression earned;
- supporting characters retain agency;
- Rule 6 clue fairness maintained;
- family-clean pass;
- physics/magic-accounting pass;
- audio-readability pass;
- state/clue ledgers updated.

## B-009 — Act Audit
At approximately 25%, 50%, and 75%:
- pacing;
- chapter-density distribution;
- protagonist dominance check;
- school/ordinary-life density;
- romance pacing;
- clue sufficiency;
- antagonist/faction pressure;
- supporting arc health.

## B-010 — Full Manuscript Word Count
Exact chapter-by-chapter count; flag outliers rather than blindly normalizing them.

## B-011 — Density Audit
Ask whether any chapter is treatment-density: lots of beats, few lived scenes.
Expand scenes, not sentences.

## B-012 — Whole-Book Continuity Audit
Timeline, injuries, ranks, travel, rules, names, relationships, secrets, promises, reveal order.

## B-013 — Rule 6 Reveal Audit
For every payoff: identify at least two pre-payoff clues and verify they occur before the reveal in prose.

## B-014 — Family-Clean Audit
Automated lint where possible + manual editorial read.

## B-015 — Audio-First Audit
TTS/read-aloud sample every chapter; check names, attribution, action geography, repeated phrases, sentence rhythm.

## B-016 — Listening Fix Pass
Change prose only for findings the audio/read-aloud pass actually exposes.

## B-017 — Copyedit
Grammar, punctuation, consistency, capitalization, formatting, typo sweep.

## B-018 — Production Lock
Record exact source commit/SHA; freeze prose except emergency correction.

## B-019 — Audio Build
Narration source generation, pronunciation sheet, renders, QC, mastering.

## B-020 — Release Package
Metadata, description, cover brief, series positioning, credits, accessibility, file exports, archival source tag.

## B-021 — Post-Release Canon Reconciliation
Update universe timeline, state ledger, spoiler matrix and future-series dependencies with only facts actually published.

# 6. CHAPTER-LEVEL MICRO-KANBAN

Each chapter card should move through:

`PLANNED → PASS 1 STRUCTURE → PASS 2 DENSITY → PASS 3 CHARACTER/CLUE → PASS 4 AUDIO → ATTESTED → AUDIO QC → LOCKED`

### Definition of ATTESTED
A chapter is not attested merely because it is “good.” It must:
- fulfill its architecture brief;
- be near expected production density;
- contain real scenes instead of compressed summaries where scenes are required;
- maintain character voice;
- earn progression;
- preserve agency;
- obey power accounting;
- plant/pay clues fairly;
- remain family-clean;
- read clearly aloud.

For long-running production, repeat:

1. Pull highest-priority READY ticket.
2. Confirm upstream canon/dependencies.
3. Perform the work completely.
4. Update ledgers / architecture / ticket status in the same commit where practical.
5. Run the relevant gate.

9. Never merge/canon-lock solely because a draft exists.

# 14. READER CONTRACT & INDEPENDENT CONVERGENCE ARCHITECTURE

**Status:** LOCKED design principle for universe planning. Exact implementation remains PROVISIONAL until each origin trilogy is architected.

## Core reader promise
The universe has a recommended reading order, but **origin trilogies are not homework for one another**.

A reader should be able to stop at multiple natural points and feel the story kept its promise:
- after **3 books**: one origin trilogy feels complete;
- after **any partial run such as 6, 9, 12, or 13 books**: every completed trilogy still feels complete and the larger pattern feels like optional enrichment rather than missing explanation;
- after **15 origin books**: the five-origin cycle is satisfying as a major saga even if the ensemble books did not exist;
- after **Ensemble Book 1**: a new reader can understand and enjoy the band forming without having read all fifteen origin books;
- after the full planned line: connections reveal a larger story that was present all along.

### Comprehension rule
Prior reading may increase **emotional depth, irony, recognition, and payoff**. It must not be required for basic scene comprehension.

When an origin lead enters an ensemble book, a new reader must understand from current-story context:
- who this person is now;
- what they can do at the level needed for the scene;
- what they want now;
- which current relationship matters;
- what immediate history is necessary.

The origin trilogy reader receives deeper meaning, not required instructions.

---

## Independent convergence model
Each origin trilogy follows its own local truth far enough that it independently reaches the same larger Boundary problem.

**Do not use a recruiter/collector as the primary mechanism that assembles five franchise leads.** The convergence should feel causal and inevitable because each protagonist solved their own trilogy honestly.

Each origin trilogy must:
1. resolve its local three-book emotional arc;
2. resolve its primary trilogy-scale antagonist/problem sufficiently for closure;
3. answer its own major promised mysteries;
4. independently discover one face of the larger shared problem;
5. end with a larger door opening, not with the local story being withheld.

### Ending principle
The emotional effect should be:

> I solved the problem I was promised.
> Now I understand that problem belonged to something much larger.

Not:

> My trilogy did not actually end; buy twelve other books to learn what happened.

---

## Five-origin convergence payload
Before any origin trilogy is canon locked, assign it a **unique convergence contribution**.

Provisional categories may include:
- identifies the larger phenomenon;
- identifies a location / destination;
- discovers a method of access or travel;
- discovers what is waiting / responding;
- discovers why convergence is dangerous but necessary.

These categories are **not yet assigned to specific leads** and may change. The locked rule is that no one trilogy should contain all five answers.

### Gate
The ensemble mission should become reconstructable only when multiple origin contributions are combined.

---

## Preferred order versus required order
Maintain two explicit reading modes.

### SOLO MODE
Choose an origin protagonist and read that three-book trilogy.

Reader receives:
- complete protagonist arc;
- complete local setting/ability promise;
- trilogy-scale ending;
- optional hints of a larger universe.

### UNIVERSE MODE
Follow recommended publication order.

Reader receives:
- accumulating crossover clues;
- recurring symbols/events/organizations;
- dramatic irony from previous trilogies;
- recognition when apparently unrelated ability traditions describe the same deeper physics;
- stronger emotional payoff when leads and counterparts finally meet.

**Release metadata should clearly distinguish recommended order from required order.**

---

## Crossover seam rules
Prefer small, optional seams over mandatory embedded novellas about future leads.

Good seams:
- news report from another origin setting;
- background scientific paper or researcher;
- recurring company/foundation/institution;
- artifact, symbol, frequency, countdown, location, star map, numerical sequence;
- unexplained rescue/disaster;
- side character crossing settings for a native reason;
- unnamed/low-context cameo that becomes meaningful only on reread;
- contradictory historical claims that another trilogy later contextualizes.

Use “book within a book” / extended embedded future-lead material only when it is **necessary to the current book's emotional story**, never as franchise advertising.

### Gate
Removing a crossover seam must not break the local plot. If removing it breaks comprehension, it is no longer a seam; it is a dependency and must be justified explicitly.

---

## Ensemble Book 1 entry-point test
Ensemble Book 1 must function as a legitimate new series opener.

A reader with zero origin-trilogy knowledge should be able to:
- follow the central mission/problem;
- distinguish the five leads and their primary counterparts;
- understand current loyalties/conflicts;
- understand each ability well enough for action stakes;
- care about the emerging band;
- finish the book without needing a wiki.

The book may intentionally leave earlier personal history unexplained when it is not relevant to the present scene.

### Backlist reward
Ensemble Book 1 should naturally make new readers curious about individual leads, allowing them to move backward into whichever origin trilogy interests them most.

---

## Stop-point satisfaction audits
Run these portfolio audits before release architecture is locked.

### R-001 — Three-Book Satisfaction Test
For each origin trilogy: if no other Boundary Universe book existed, does Book 3 feel like a complete ending?

### R-002 — Partial-Universe Satisfaction Test
Test plausible stopping points, including a reader who has completed approximately 13 origin-cycle books. Verify no incomplete trilogy or unrelated series is required to understand what they have already read.

### R-003 — Fifteen-Book Origin-Cycle Test
If the ensemble saga were canceled, would the five origin trilogies together still feel like a large, satisfying connected saga with deliberate larger mysteries rather than an unfinished crossover setup?

### R-004 — Ensemble Cold-Entry Test
Give Ensemble Book 1's architecture/manuscript to a hypothetical reader with no origin context. Flag every place the text assumes homework.

### R-005 — Full-Universe Reread Test
Track connections that gain meaning after later books. Reward full-series reading through reinterpretation, not through retroactively supplying missing basics.

---

## Spoiler ownership rule
Every shared-universe reveal has one primary owning story or cycle.

A different trilogy may:
- clue it;
- misinterpret it;
- observe consequences;
- contain a partial version.

It should not accidentally steal another trilogy's earned reveal.

The Master Timeline & Spoiler Matrix must include:
- reveal owner;
- first clue;
- second independent clue;
- earliest safe explicit mention;
- origin-cycle safe interpretation;
- ensemble interpretation;
- prequel reinterpretation if applicable.

---

## Reader-contract failure conditions
Block release architecture if any of these are true:
- an origin trilogy ends mainly as setup for another protagonist's books;
- a protagonist's emotional climax occurs outside their own trilogy;
- Ensemble Book 1 requires biographies from multiple origin trilogies to understand the immediate plot;
- a crossover cameo displaces the current protagonist's supporting cast;
- a prequel contains information necessary to understand an already-released origin ending;
- one origin trilogy carries so much shared cosmology that the others become optional appendices rather than equal doors into the universe;
- the recommended reading order becomes a disguised required order.

---

## Current architectural intent
The five protagonists should feel, from inside their solo stories, as though they are following unrelated truths.

By the ends of their trilogies, each should have independently reached a different side of the same door.

The ensemble saga begins when those independently earned discoveries become impossible to solve separately.
