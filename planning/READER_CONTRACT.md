# BOUNDARY UNIVERSE — READER CONTRACT

**Status:** LOCKED design principle; implementation details remain PROVISIONAL until individual trilogy architectures are attested.

## Promise
The Boundary Universe has a recommended reading order, but origin trilogies are not homework for one another.

A reader should be satisfied after:
- one complete origin trilogy (3 books);
- a partial origin-cycle run such as 6, 9, 12, or 13 books;
- all 15 origin books, even if no ensemble saga existed;
- Ensemble Book 1 as a legitimate new-series entry point;
- the full planned publication line, where rereading exposes a larger hidden story.

Prior reading may increase emotional depth and recognition. It must not be required for basic comprehension.

## Independent convergence
Each origin protagonist resolves their own trilogy, then independently discovers one face of the same larger Boundary problem.

Do not primarily assemble the heroes through a collector/recruiter mechanism. Their separate truths should lead them naturally to the same larger door.

Each origin ending must provide:
1. local emotional closure;
2. local external closure;
3. payoff of promised trilogy mysteries;
4. one unique convergence contribution;
5. a larger door opening after the promised story has already been paid.

## Crossover seams
Prefer optional seams: news, papers, artifacts, institutions, symbols, unexplained events, cameos, contradictory histories, shared measurements, or recurring locations.

Removing a crossover seam should not break the local plot.

Extended embedded stories about future protagonists are allowed only when necessary to the current book's own emotional story; never use them as franchise advertising.

## Ensemble entry
Ensemble Book 1 must work for a reader who has read none of the origin trilogies.

It must establish present-tense identity, abilities, loyalties, conflicts, and stakes without biography dumps or homework.

Origin readers receive richer subtext; new readers receive a complete current story.

## Stop-point tests
- R-001: 3-book trilogy satisfaction.
- R-002: partial-universe / ~13-book satisfaction.
- R-003: 15-book origin-cycle satisfaction.
- R-004: Ensemble Book 1 cold-entry test.
- R-005: full-universe reread/reinterpretation test.

## Failure conditions
Block release architecture if:
- an origin trilogy's emotional climax is deferred to another series;
- another origin trilogy is required to understand the ending;
- Ensemble Book 1 assumes multiple biographies;
- a prequel repairs missing exposition from a released origin trilogy;
- crossover cameos displace native supporting casts;
- one origin line owns so much cosmology that the others stop feeling like equal entry points;
- recommended order becomes a disguised required order.
