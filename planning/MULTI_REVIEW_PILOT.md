# MULTI-REVIEW PILOT — BOOK 2

**Status:** READY TO PILOT
**Branch:** `draft/book-02-four-pass`
**Governing orchestration:** `planning/MULTI_REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION.md` on `planning/universe-kanban`

## Purpose
Use *The Twelfth Resonant* as the first real pilot of the autonomous multi-review loop before promoting the orchestration policy to LOCKED governance.

## Pilot scope
Start at the next unattested Book 2 chapter and run:

1. primary four-pass drafting;
2. independent structural/character review;
3. independent canon/clue review;
4. reconciliation;
5. targeted revision;
6. independent attestation;
7. ledger update;
8. automatic promotion of next chapter ticket.

For one high-risk reveal chapter later in the book, add a third adversarial review.

## Pilot measurements
Track:
- number of material findings per reviewer;
- overlap between reviewers;
- contradictory findings;
- findings accepted/rejected/deferred;
- whether independent review catches issues self-review missed;
- whether reconciliation causes unnecessary prose churn;
- whether ticket state and SHA provenance are sufficient for a fresh reviewer;
- number of times creator intervention is genuinely required;
- whether chapter-to-chapter handoff remains coherent;
- whether the loop naturally advances without hidden conversation state.

## Success criteria
The pilot succeeds if:
- independent review materially improves at least one chapter without destabilizing canon;
- every finding can be traced to a reviewer and reviewed SHA;
- reconciliation is explicit;
- next-ticket promotion works without ambiguity;
- creator escalation remains limited to genuinely open creative choices;
- no model is required to remember uncommitted context from another model/session.

## Governance promotion
After a successful Book 2 pilot, update `MULTI_REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION.md` from PROVISIONAL GOVERNANCE to LOCKED (or revise it based on pilot findings) before using autonomous loops across Origins 2–5.
